YOUTUBE BANNER — MASTER PRODUCTION PROMPT

Create ONE finished YouTube channel banner at exactly:

2560 × 1440 pixels

The finished product must be ONE clean PNG ready to upload to YouTube.

This is a TWO-STAGE production workflow.

Do not combine the stages.

==================================================
STAGE 1 — GENERATE THE BACKGROUND ONLY
==================================================

Use image generation ONLY to create the scenic background.

Generate ONE beautiful cinematic image of an empty grand opera theater viewed from the stage.

Visual direction:

- elegant red velvet curtains
- warm theatrical spotlights
- ornate balconies
- rows of red theater seats
- polished wooden stage
- warm gold and deep-red palette
- dramatic but inviting lighting
- symmetrical composition
- realistic/cinematic appearance
- no people

CRITICAL:

The generated background must contain absolutely:

- NO words
- NO letters
- NO title
- NO subtitle
- NO ticket
- NO signs
- NO logos
- NO stamps
- NO graphic panels
- NO text of any kind

Generate ONE background only.

Once a clean background exists, STOP using image generation.

Do not ask the image generator to create the finished banner.

==================================================
STAGE 2 — BUILD THE BANNER PROGRAMMATICALLY
==================================================

Use Python/Pillow or another true programmatic image-compositing method for ALL remaining work.

Resize/crop the clean theater background to exactly:

2560 × 1440 pixels

Then add the typography, divider, star, ticket, sizing, and positioning PROGRAMMATICALLY.

Do not use an image generator for any of these elements.

==================================================
THEATER BACKGROUND MUST REMAIN CONTINUOUS
==================================================

The theater photograph must remain visually continuous across the entire 2560 × 1440 banner.

The safe area is an INVISIBLE positioning constraint.

It must NEVER become part of the visible design.

ABSOLUTELY DO NOT create:

- a black rectangle
- a dark rectangle
- a translucent rectangle
- a panel
- a card
- a banner inside the banner
- a vignette box
- a tinted safe area
- a blurred rectangle
- a gradient box
- a background plate
- a framed center section
- any other visible representation of the safe area

Do not darken, lighten, blur, tint, mask, or otherwise visually distinguish the safe area from the surrounding theater.

The exact same continuous theater image must remain visible behind and around the lettering.

For readability, ONLY the individual letters themselves may receive subtle:

- shadow
- stroke
- outline
- glow

The SOLD OUT ticket may have its own cream-colored ticket background because the ticket itself is a physical graphic element.

==================================================
ESSENTIAL CONTENT
==================================================

Programmatically add exactly:

It's Time To Start The Show

Then:

short gold line — small gold star — short gold line

Then:

A Love Story Opera

and a small vintage ticket reading:

SOLD OUT
to Jesus

==================================================
LAYOUT
==================================================

All essential content must remain comfortably inside YouTube's central "Viewable on all devices" safe region.

However:

DO NOT FILL THE SAFE AREA.

The safe area is merely the maximum survival region.

Make the actual title/subtitle/ticket composition substantially SMALLER than the safe area.

Leave generous empty space:

- above the title
- below the lower row
- to the left
- to the right

The composition should be a compact centered group.

Use approximately this arrangement:

             It's Time To Start The Show

                    —— ✦ ——

       A Love Story Opera     [ SOLD OUT
                                  to Jesus ]

The title must remain on ONE line.

The title is the dominant element.

The divider is decorative and secondary.

The subtitle is smaller than the title.

The SOLD OUT ticket is small and secondary.

The subtitle and ticket should form a balanced lower row.

Do not enlarge any element simply because unused safe-area space remains.

==================================================
IMPORTANT YOUTUBE PREVIEW CONSIDERATION
==================================================

YouTube may display floating interface labels such as:

"Viewable on TV"
"Viewable on desktop"
"Viewable on all devices"

These are temporary YouTube UI overlays.

They are NOT part of the banner.
They are NOT safe-area boundaries.
They are NOT artwork.

Do not interpret the edges of these labels as crop boundaries.

Because the "Viewable on all devices" label may temporarily cover the upper-left portion of the center region during upload preview, keep the title comfortably centered with generous space around it.

The composition should still be understandable even while that temporary YouTube label is visible.

1. Final image dimensions are exactly 2560 × 1440.

3. All typography and the ticket were added programmatically after background generation.

4. The title remains on one line.

8. There is NO visible safe-area rectangle, panel, card, vignette, tint, mask, gradient box, or background plate.

9. The continuous theater photograph remains visible directly behind the typography.

10. Only individual text effects and the physical ticket itself may alter the pixels immediately surrounding those elements.

DO NOT DELIVER THE IMAGE.

Correct it programmatically and test again.
