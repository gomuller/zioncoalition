CRITICAL GEOMETRY — MY ACTUAL YOUTUBE SCREENSHOT IS AUTHORITATIVE
==================================================

4. If I provide a screenshot of YouTube's actual "Customize banner art" upload preview, that screenshot is the FINAL AUTHORITY for the All Devices crop.

DO NOT use a generic published safe-area rectangle instead of my screenshot.

DO NOT reuse previously assumed coordinates such as:

1450 × 380

unless those exact values are freshly derived from the current screenshot.

5. Measure the actual YouTube preview screenshot programmatically.

Identify:

A. The complete displayed banner rectangle.
B. The inner rectangle labeled "Viewable on all devices" or "All devices."

Measure the pixel coordinates of both rectangles in the screenshot.

Then convert the inner rectangle from screenshot coordinates into NORMALIZED coordinates relative to the displayed banner:

safe_left_ratio   = (safe_left - banner_left) / banner_width
safe_right_ratio  = (safe_right - banner_left) / banner_width
safe_top_ratio    = (safe_top - banner_top) / banner_height
safe_bottom_ratio = (safe_bottom - banner_top) / banner_height

Then translate those ratios onto the exact 2560 × 1440 master canvas:

MASTER_SAFE_LEFT   = round(safe_left_ratio × 2560)
MASTER_SAFE_RIGHT  = round(safe_right_ratio × 2560)
MASTER_SAFE_TOP    = round(safe_top_ratio × 1440)
MASTER_SAFE_BOTTOM = round(safe_bottom_ratio × 1440)

Do not eyeball this conversion.

Do not infer it from memory.

Actually calculate it.

6. After calculating the screenshot-derived master safe rectangle, create an even SMALLER internal content box.

Inset the screenshot-derived safe rectangle by approximately:

5% of its width on the left
5% of its width on the right
5% of its height on the top
5% of its height on the bottom

Call this:

HARD_CONTENT_BOX

ALL essential foreground content must fit inside HARD_CONTENT_BOX.

This deliberate inset provides extra protection from measurement error, UI scaling, antialiasing, and text edges.

7. If no current YouTube crop-preview screenshot is supplied, then and only then verify YouTube's current official specifications and derive the safe area from them.

But whenever my actual YouTube upload screenshot exists:
