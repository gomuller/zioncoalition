THE KEY LESSON FROM OUR SUCCESSFUL TEST
==================================================

The entire upload must still be 2560 × 1440.

BUT:

The important recognizable visual composition must be deliberately fitted into the narrow CENTER 1546 × 423 strip.

This includes:

- the important part of the theater
- title
- subtitle
- decorative divider/star
- SOLD OUT ticket

Think of the 1546 × 423 center strip as the REAL banner.

Think of the rest of the 2560 × 1440 canvas as expendable extension/background that YouTube may crop away on different devices.

THIS DISTINCTION IS CRITICAL.

Do NOT simply scale a normal 2560 × 1440 composition and place text somewhere near the center.

Instead, intentionally construct the meaningful banner composition at 1546 × 423 and place that composition in the exact center of the 2560 × 1440 upload canvas.
