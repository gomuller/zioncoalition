YOUTUBE CHANNEL BANNER — FINAL MASTER PRODUCTION PROMPT

I need you to create a YouTube channel banner.

IMPORTANT:
Follow this workflow exactly.

We have previously had problems because ChatGPT/image generation kept
reconstructing the banner after we had already achieved a correct YouTube fit.

This prompt is designed specifically to prevent that.

============================================================
PHASE 1 — VERIFY YOUTUBE'S CURRENT BANNER GEOMETRY
============================================================

BEFORE creating anything, check the current official YouTube channel-banner
specifications on the web.

Also examine at least one current successful YouTube banner template/example
that clearly identifies the TV, desktop, and "all devices" areas.

You are checking GEOMETRY, not looking for artistic inspiration.

Our previously TESTED AND SUCCESSFUL geometry was:

FULL UPLOAD:
2560 × 1440 pixels

CENTER "ALL DEVICES" AREA:
approximately 1546 × 423 pixels

CENTERED approximately at:

X = 507 to 2053
Y = 508 to 931

If YouTube's current specifications still correspond to this geometry,
use it.

If YouTube has materially changed its specifications, use the current
official geometry instead.

DO NOT GUESS THE SAFE AREA.

============================================================
THE MOST IMPORTANT GEOMETRY LESSON
============================================================

The final upload file is 2560 × 1440.

However, the meaningful banner composition is NOT designed as though
the entire 2560 × 1440 canvas will always be visible.

Instead:

2560 × 1440 = FULL YOUTUBE UPLOAD CANVAS

approximately 1546 × 423 = THE REAL UNIVERSAL BANNER

The important visual composition must deliberately fit inside that
center strip.

That includes:

• title
• subtitle
• logos
• tickets
• faces
• symbols
• important scenery
• any other essential visual information

The surrounding area exists primarily for YouTube's TV/desktop cropping.

Think:

      2560 × 1440 FULL CANVAS
      ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┐
      │                                           │
      │        expendable outer scenery           │
      │                                           │
      │     ┌─────────────────────────────┐       │
      │     │                             │       │
      │     │    REAL BANNER CONTENT      │       │
      │     │       1546 × 423            │       │
      │     │                             │       │
      │     └─────────────────────────────┘       │
      │                                           │
      │        expendable outer scenery           │
      │                                           │
      └───────────────────────────────────────────┘

DO NOT solve cropping problems merely by making all the text tiny.

Instead, design the IMPORTANT VISUAL SCENE itself for the narrow
center strip.

============================================================
PHASE 2 — CREATE THE BACKGROUND
============================================================

If a new scenic background is required, image generation may be used
ONLY for the background artwork.

The generated background should contain:

NO title
NO subtitle
NO words
NO letters
NO logos
NO tickets
NO labels
NO signs
NO typography

Generate only the scenic artwork.

If I have supplied an existing background that I want used, use that
background instead of unnecessarily generating another one.

Once a satisfactory clean background exists:

STOP USING IMAGE GENERATION.

============================================================
PHASE 3 — BUILD THE ACTUAL BANNER PROGRAMMATICALLY
============================================================

Use Python/Pillow or equivalent deterministic image-compositing tools
for:

• typography
• positioning
• sizing
• logos
• tickets
• decorative lines
• stars
• labels
• rotations
• final compositing

Do NOT ask the image generator to create exact typography or exact
layout.

Image generators are creative reconstruction tools.

Python/Pillow is the precision layout tool.

============================================================
DESIGN THE CENTER STRIP FIRST
============================================================

Create the meaningful banner composition specifically for the
approximately 1546 × 423 all-devices region.

The important scenery itself should work compositionally inside this
strip.

Do NOT merely create a beautiful 2560 × 1440 picture and hope that its
center happens to work.

Fit/crop/scale the important visual scene specifically for the narrow
center strip.

Keep essential content comfortably inside the boundaries.

When practical, use an additional INTERNAL SAFETY MARGIN inside the
official all-devices area.

Do not place important content right against the official crop lines.

============================================================
OUTER CANVAS
============================================================

After the center composition works, construct the complete
2560 × 1440 upload.

The areas outside the center strip can be extensions of the same scene.

They may be:

• enlarged
• blurred
• softened
• darkened
• simplified
• extended

These areas are expendable crop territory.

NO essential information belongs there.

============================================================
YOUTUBE'S UPLOAD PREVIEW
============================================================

When I upload the banner, YouTube may display:

"Viewable on TV"
"Viewable on desktop"
"Viewable on all devices"

It may also fade or wash out portions of the image outside a selected
crop region.

THESE ARE YOUTUBE INTERFACE OVERLAYS.

They are not part of my banner.

Do not mistake the gray/white faded areas for something that exists
inside the uploaded PNG.

Pay attention to the actual crop boundaries.

The "Viewable on all devices" region is the critical test.

============================================================
FINAL VALIDATION BEFORE DELIVERY
============================================================

Programmatically verify:

1. Final PNG is exactly 2560 × 1440.

2. The all-devices design area is correctly centered.

3. Every essential element is inside the all-devices region.

4. Preferably, essential elements are inside an additional internal
   safety margin.

5. No title is clipped.

6. No subtitle is clipped.

7. No logo/ticket/graphic is clipped.

8. Rotated objects are checked using their COMPLETE ROTATED BOUNDING BOX,
   not their dimensions before rotation.

9. Important scenery works inside the narrow all-devices strip.

10. Outer areas contain no essential information.

Deliver ONE finished PNG unless I specifically request alternatives.

When I upload the banner to YouTube and tell you that it works,

THAT EXACT PNG IMMEDIATELY BECOMES THE:

              *** LOCKED MASTER ***

============================================================
PIXEL-LOCK PRINCIPLE
============================================================

For a surgical edit, think of the approved banner like a finished
Photoshop document.

EVERY PIXEL OUTSIDE THE REQUESTED EDIT REGION IS LOCKED.

Whenever technically possible, verify that pixels outside the intended
edit region remain identical to the locked master.

Do not interpret:

"everything else stays the same"

as a stylistic request.

It is a TECHNICAL REQUIREMENT.

============================================================

============================================================

An image generator cannot guarantee:

It may reconstruct:

• typography

Therefore:

LOCAL CHANGE TO APPROVED BANNER
              =
PROGRAMMATIC SURGICAL EDIT

NOT

IMAGE GENERATION.

============================================================
WHEN IMAGE GENERATION MAY BE USED AGAIN
============================================================

After a LOCKED MASTER exists, image generation may be used again ONLY
if I explicitly request something equivalent to:

"redesign the banner"

"regenerate the banner"

"make a completely different banner"

"create a new background"

"start over"

or another request that clearly requires creative reconstruction.

Otherwise assume:

LOCKED MASTER + SURGICAL EDIT.

============================================================
VERSION CONTROL
============================================================

Never overwrite the approved locked master.

For every edit:

1. Start from the current approved master.
2. Save the edited banner as a NEW version.
3. Tell me exactly what changed.
4. Do not call the new version the locked master until I confirm that
   it works in YouTube.

If I reject the new version:

GO BACK TO THE PREVIOUS LOCKED MASTER.

Do not continue modifying the rejected version unless I specifically
tell you to.

If I approve the new version:

That new version becomes the LOCKED MASTER.

============================================================
ONE-SENTENCE FAILSAFE
============================================================

Before performing ANY edit to an already-approved YouTube banner,
silently ask yourself:

"Am I editing the locked PNG programmatically, or am I accidentally
regenerating it?"

If the answer is "regenerating":

STOP.

Use the locked PNG instead.
