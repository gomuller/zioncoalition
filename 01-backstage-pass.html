Most of what I do publicly is FRONT STAGE.

Facebook posts.
Jesus Street Preaching.
Zion Coalition.

My Jesus-Greg WORLD.
Whatever this thing is becoming.

Increasingly, I am trying to let the art just happen in front of you without immediately explaining it.

That is intentional.

🎵 “I’m trying to be like Jesus.”

Jesus had a habit of doing and saying things FRONT STAGE that people did not immediately understand. Parables. Strange sayings. Symbolic acts. Questions instead of answers.

Sometimes the explanation came later.

Sometimes it apparently didn't.

So I am experimenting with that too.

FRONT STAGE

On the FRONT STAGE, you might encounter me casually referring to my “Bible,” inventing a word like presenced-upon, talking about Jesus TV, rehearsing an opera, turning my house into scripture, redeeming a classic-rock song, or behaving as though Jesus is an active participant in the performance.

And I may not explain myself.

The absence of explanation is becoming part of the art.

But there is another door.

BACK STAGE

A small group of people have indicated that they actually want to know what is going on behind the curtain.

Questionable judgment, perhaps.

Nevertheless:

BACK STAGE is where I explain the experiment.

Occasionally I send Notes from the Artist—emails in which I pull back the curtain (open the veil?) and talk about what I think, believe, imagine, wonder, guess, experiment with, and am trying to do.

It is the commentary track.

The footnotes.

The deleted scenes.

The director talking over the movie.

The machinery behind the stage.

The Book of MORE Jesus, perhaps.

Bonus, extra-biblical material.

FRONT STAGE is the art.

BACK STAGE is Greg trying to explain what Greg thinks might have just happened.

Oompah, oompah.
