BLACK & LESS BLACK

00-15 · Continuous Text Art · Jesus-Greg Master Prompt

Activation

“Black and less black this:” followed by the authoritative text. This is a standalone technique: pillow, parchment, green, or any other requested background.

NO SPACES BETWEEN WORDS. EVERY SUCCESSIVE WORD CHANGES SHADE. NO EXCEPTIONS.

Mandatory production rules

  1. Verbatim source: Preserve every word, spelling, capitalization, punctuation, apostrophe, ellipsis, hyphen, and repetition. Remove interword whitespace only; attach punctuation to its original word. Do not transcribe generated artwork as the source.
  2. Strict alternation: Word 1 near-black #111111; word 2 visibly lighter charcoal #777777; word 3 near-black; word 4 charcoal; continue throughout the entire piece. Do not restart at lines, sentences, or paragraphs. The shade change must occur at each original word boundary, even though the visible space is removed.
  3. Never substitute artistic emphasis: No randomly highlighted words, no color changes every phrase, and no decorative exceptions. The alternation is structural, not optional.
  4. Background flexibility: Use the user's requested parchment, modern green, pillow, or other background. Maintain enough contrast for BOTH shades to remain unmistakably distinct from each other and readable against the background. Adjust the lighter tone if needed for accessibility while retaining the dark/light rhythm.
  5. Typography: Dense, legible serif text for posters unless a different font is requested. No visible spaces between words; line wrapping is allowed. Use ample margins and enough image height to avoid clipping or tiny text.
  6. Deterministic rendering preferred: For exact results, split the original text into words, assign alternating colors to individual word spans, remove interword gaps, and render the composition via SVG, HTML/CSS, or equivalent. Do not rely on image generation to guarantee letter-perfect text or consistent alternation.
  7. Mandatory inspection: Before delivery inspect the beginning, middle, and end. Confirm dark/light/dark/light at every consecutive word boundary, no restarts, no clipped text, and faithful wording. Correct failures before presenting the artwork. Never claim perfect verification unless performed.
  8. Revision discipline: Keep the last authoritative text separately from the last approved visual master. Apply only the user's specified changes. New background instructions must not weaken the alternation rule.

Example

Original: “Without vision, the people perish.”

Withoutvision,thepeopleperish.

The colors indicate original word boundaries without adding spaces.

Quality-control priority

1. Exact source text. 2. Strict word-by-word shade alternation. 3. No visible interword spaces. 4. Background and decorative styling.

A generative picture may approximate the effect but cannot ensure exact lettering or exact alternation. Prefer a deterministic typographic overlay when precision matters.

The writing is the picture. The picture is the writing.
