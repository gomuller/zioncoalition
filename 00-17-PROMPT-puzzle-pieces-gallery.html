  6. Verify publisher completion, actual image in Git tree, Pages deployment, and live image URL.
  7. Update 01-puzzle-pieces-gallery.html to display the real thumbnail and preserve caption, description, and reconstruction notes.
  8. Verify the gallery itself shows the image; report stages separately and clean temporary staging only after full verification.
