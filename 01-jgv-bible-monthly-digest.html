Why a monthly digest?

Jesus doesn't send me all the new parts of this Bible at once. As I experience and understand it, new thoughts, revelations, allusions, annotations, art pieces, and old-man dreams arrive a little at a time—sometimes so abundantly that even I can scarcely keep up with the cataloguing.

Joel 2:28 speaks of old men dreaming dreams. Here I am, an old man dreaming dreams, trusting that Jesus has a wise purpose in what He is sending down.

The revelations may come daily. The invitations come monthly. The Bible keeps growing.

If I pushed every new addition immediately, my seven mortal subscribers might soon feel inundated. And what of the multitude of immortal watchers? I suppose they have somewhat greater capacities for keeping up.

So instead of a daily push, I am experimenting with one invitation at the end of each month. Smaller art pieces may still appear on Facebook or arrive by personal text. Those are glimpses through the windows. The monthly digest opens the door to the larger rooms.
