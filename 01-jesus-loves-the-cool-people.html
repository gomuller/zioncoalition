Jesus Loves the Cool People

A love note from the manger scene to the INNkeepers

← Return to the Jesus Carrot · Podium Museum

I love cool people.

I have long wished I was one of the cool people.

But, alas, I have never been “cool”. And I doubt that I will ever earn cool people membership.

It's not from lack of trying.

I used to knock, knock, knock, on the “cool people” door. “Let me in I'm cool.”. I was hoping that once they let me in, and got to know me (the real Greg) that then they would discover how cool I am, and they would make me an official member of the cool people.

Nope.

When the cool people finally open the door to me they quite quickly detect that I am just a wannabe, not a member of their cool people tribe.

No room in the inn. For me.

And that is by intelligent design.

For, when I was born again in 2015, Jesus told me (or basically told me),

“Greg, stop knocking on the cool-people's door. You don't belong there. Obviously. You are not cool. Your place and your people is with me (and the sheep. and the cows. and the straw bales. and the manure.), here in the manger scene, not the cool people INNkeeper scene.”

Jesus explained further to me that the cool people are foreordained INNkeepers of society,
“Greg, the cool people are called from before the foundations of the earth to be members of the INN crowd people, the purveyors of what is INN vogue, the INN dispensable, the INN portant, INN control, INN fluencers, and such.”

Turns out, Jesus loves the cool people. A lot.

That, of course, is obvious.

It is made obvious by the fact that Jesus blesses cool people variously with a lot of talent, and/or money, and/or prestige, and/or beauty, and/or power, and/or other things that the foreordained “cool person” have.

In spades.

Most of us readily know who the cool people are. We knew who they were in high school. We know who they are at church. We know who they are at work. We know who they are on TV. We know who they are in the neighborhood and in our community.

And… by and large, they know who they are too. They know they're in the club.

A still small voice seems to say to me, “Greg, I am equally with the cool people and the not-cool people. I love both groups equally. You need to appreciate the cool people more, in a deep, kind way. For the cool people's burden is often heavy. It's not easy being cool and holy. But I am well pleased with many cool people. They are good INNkeepers; They suffer long. They are kind. They are good. They repent. And they (often privately) have the spirit of the Manger Scene continually burning in their hearts.”

So, Jesus loves the cool people.

I do too.

Many of them have been kind to me over the years.

This is my love note to the cool people, of who's tribe I am not, but respect, more and more, as I get MORE and MORE Jesus.

From Greg's original writing, “999 movie Jesus Loves The Cool People.”
