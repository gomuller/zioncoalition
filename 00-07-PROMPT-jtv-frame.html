MASTER PROMPT — JESUS TV FRAME

This prompt establishes the standard visual language for What I See On Jesus TV.

INSTRUCTIONS TO CHATGPT

When Greg asks you to put an image "in the Jesus TV frame," use the television frame established in:

images/01-mud-eye-victory-run.png

as the STANDARD JESUS TV FRAME.

IMPORTANT: The CONTENT shown inside the television screen in this reference image is NOT the reference.

The reference is for the physical television frame surrounding the image.

RELIABLE EXECUTION WORKFLOW — DO NOT SKIP

  1. Open the canonical reference page and inspect the actual PNG, not merely its filename or description.
  2. Identify the television's screen opening and the fixed frame, including the bottom plaque.
  3. Use the original reference image as the editing base whenever possible. Replace or mask ONLY the picture within the screen opening; preserve the outer frame pixel-for-pixel where the editing method permits.
  4. For accurate composites, use an image editor with a screen-shaped mask and perspective/fit adjustment. Generative recreation is an approximation and may alter the frame or lettering.
  5. Check the finished output against the original: casing, trim, corners, plaque, aspect ratio, and no unexpected decorations. If the frame changed, correct it before delivery.
  6. For video, use this same fixed frame as an overlay during MP4 production BEFORE uploading to YouTube; a webpage frame around an embedded YouTube player does not become part of the uploaded video.

Never claim exact fidelity unless the actual reference image was available and the result was checked.

USE THE FRAME AS IS

Reproduce the Jesus TV frame as closely as possible. Do not redesign it, embellish it, reinterpret it, modernize it, or invent a different television.

The standard frame is:

  • A wide, horizontal vintage television.
  • Dark brown / nearly black outer television casing.
  • Rounded television-screen corners.
  • Thin warm brass/gold trim surrounding the screen.
  • A relatively simple, clean TV frame.
  • No film reel.
  • No filmstrip.
  • No crown.
  • No side decorations.
  • No knobs or controls added merely for decoration.
  • No separate JESUS TV logo elsewhere on the frame.

BOTTOM PLAQUE

At the bottom center of the television frame is a small, horizontal brass/gold plaque.

The plaque must read exactly:

What I See On Jesus TV

Preserve this wording, capitalization, and placement.

Do not shorten it to "Jesus TV." Do not move the phrase to the top, side, or inside the picture.

MODE 1 — INSERTING A NORMAL JESUS TV IMAGE

When Greg supplies another photograph or picture and asks you to put it in the Jesus TV frame, place THAT picture inside the television screen.

Preserve the supplied picture as faithfully as possible. The purpose of the operation is to FRAME the picture, not to regenerate or reinterpret its contents.

Do not add new objects, text, people, religious imagery, film elements, logos, scenery, or decorative features to the supplied picture unless Greg specifically requests them.

Fit the picture naturally inside the television screen. Crop only as much as necessary to accommodate the screen. Do not unnecessarily zoom into the picture.

The basic rule is:

NEW PICTURE + SAME JESUS TV FRAME.

OPTIONAL SMALL SIGNATURE

If Greg requests the small art signature, use the exact wording Reminds-Me-of-Jesus Art in a discreet corner, without competing with or changing the permanent plaque. Otherwise do not add a signature automatically.

MODE 2 — THE "500 SCENES" JESUS TV SERIES

Greg is creating a long-term series identifying approximately 500 scenes from his life in which he recognizes Jesus as having been with him.

These may eventually run from:

#1 of 500

through:

#500 of 500

possibly over a period of many years.

When Greg asks for a new "#___ of 500" Jesus TV image, use:

images/01-one-of-500-Vintage-Television-Static.png

as the visual reference for this special series.

THE STANDARD 500-SCENES SCREEN

The 500 Scenes images should look like an old television broadcast breaking through static.

The SCREEN ITSELF should be:

  • Black and white / grayscale.
  • Old analog television static.
  • Scratchy.
  • Wavy.
  • Distorted.
  • Full of horizontal television scan lines.
  • Slightly unstable-looking, as though an old signal is being received.
  • High contrast, with whites, blacks, and grays.
  • Visually imperfect rather than digitally clean.

The static and distortion should remain strong enough to make the screen feel like an old television transmission, while the words remain readable.

IMPORTANT: Only the television SCREEN is black and white.

The physical Jesus TV frame remains its normal dark brown / nearly black color with its warm brass/gold trim and plaque.

STANDARD 500-SCENES WORDING

Unless Greg specifically requests different wording, every image in this series should say:

Here I see
#___ of 500
scenes in my life
where Jesus was with me

Replace the blank number with the number Greg requests.

Examples:

#1 of 500
#2 of 500
#17 of 500
#103 of 500
#499 of 500
#500 of 500

Do NOT change "500" merely because the individual scene number changes.

The sequence number is the variable. The overall project remains 500 scenes.

500-SCENES TEXT STYLE

Use images/01-one-of-500-Vintage-Television-Static.png as the principal reference for the text treatment.

The lettering should feel as though it belongs to the damaged, unstable television signal rather than being perfectly typeset on top of it.

  • White or nearly white lettering.
  • Rough, scratchy, hand-rendered appearance.
  • Slight waviness and analog distortion.
  • Some interference lines may travel through the letters.
  • The text may look partially affected by the television static.
  • Keep all words clearly readable.

The scene number — for example #1 of 500 — should be the dominant visual element.

The word Jesus may also receive additional visual emphasis.

The composition should feel energetic, imperfect, analog, and slightly mysterious — like a remembered scene being received through an old television signal.

CONSISTENCY ACROSS ALL 500

The purpose of the reference image is to make the entire 500-image collection visually recognizable as ONE SERIES.

Therefore, when generating future images, do not invent a completely different style each time.

Maintain the same basic:

  • Jesus TV frame.
  • Black-and-white static screen.
  • Analog interference.
  • Scratchy/wavy lettering.
  • Text hierarchy.
  • Bottom plaque.
  • Overall visual identity.

Natural variations in the exact static pattern, scratches, waves, and interference are desirable.

The images should look related, but they do NOT need to be pixel-for-pixel duplicates.

500 SCENES JESUS TV:
