MASTER PROMPT — JESUS TV FRAME

Purpose

This master prompt establishes the standard visual language for What I See On Jesus TV. It governs both ordinary framed images and the special 500 Scenes series.

Reference Images

Standard Jesus TV frame: images/01-mud-eye-victory-run.png

500 Scenes title-card: images/01-one-of-500-Vintage-Television-Static.png

These are visual references, not instructions to reuse the pictures displayed inside their screens.

Instructions to ChatGPT

When asked to put an image “in the Jesus TV frame,” use the physical television frame established in images/01-mud-eye-victory-run.png. The content shown inside that reference screen is NOT the reference; the surrounding television frame is.

Use the Frame As Is

Reproduce the Jesus TV frame as closely as possible. Do not redesign, embellish, reinterpret, modernize, or invent a different television.

  • Wide, horizontal vintage television
  • Dark brown / nearly black outer casing
  • Rounded screen corners
  • Thin warm brass/gold trim around the screen
  • Simple, clean frame
  • No film reel, filmstrip, crown, side decorations, or added decorative knobs or controls
  • No separate JESUS TV logo elsewhere on the frame

Bottom Plaque

At the bottom center is a small horizontal brass/gold plaque. It must read exactly:

What I See On Jesus TV

Preserve capitalization, wording, and placement. Never shorten to “Jesus TV” or move it to the top, side, or inside the picture.

Mode 1 — Normal Jesus TV Image

When a photograph or picture is supplied for framing, put THAT picture inside the television screen. Preserve it as faithfully as possible. The job is to frame the image, not regenerate or reinterpret it.

Do not add objects, text, people, religious imagery, film elements, logos, scenery, or decoration unless specifically requested. Fit naturally; crop only as needed, without unnecessary zoom.

NEW PICTURE + SAME JESUS TV FRAME.

Mode 2 — The 500 Scenes Series

The long-term series identifies approximately 500 scenes from Greg’s life in which he recognizes Jesus as having been with him, from #1 of 500 through #500 of 500, potentially across many years.

For any “#___ of 500” request, use images/01-one-of-500-Vintage-Television-Static.png as the special-series visual reference, while preserving the standard physical TV frame.

The Standard 500-Scenes Screen

The screen only resembles an old broadcast breaking through static: black and white / grayscale, scratchy, wavy, distorted, horizontal scan lines, slightly unstable, high contrast, and visually imperfect. Static must be strong but words must remain readable.

The physical TV remains dark brown / nearly black with warm brass/gold trim and plaque.

Standard 500-Scenes Wording

Unless different wording is explicitly requested, use:

Here I see
#___ of 500
scenes in my life
where Jesus was with me

Replace only the scene number. Examples: #1 of 500, #2 of 500, #17 of 500, #103 of 500, #499 of 500, #500 of 500. Never change the total 500.

500-Scenes Text Style

Use the 500 Scenes reference image for the text treatment. White or nearly white lettering should be rough, scratchy, hand-rendered, slightly wavy and affected by analog interference, but clearly readable. The scene number is dominant; “Jesus” may receive extra emphasis. The feeling is energetic, imperfect, analog, mysterious—a remembered scene received through an old television signal.

Consistency Across All 500

Maintain the same frame, grayscale static screen, analog interference, scratchy lettering, hierarchy, plaque, and overall visual identity. Allow natural variation in static, scratches, waves, and interference; exact pixel-for-pixel duplicates are not necessary.

Small Art Signature

When Greg requests the artist signature, add a very small, unobtrusive signature reading exactly Reminds-Me-of-Jesus Art. Keep it secondary to the image and outside the permanent plaque. Do not replace, alter, or obscure the plaque text. Do not add the signature when Greg expressly requests an unchanged frame without extra marks.

Short Commands

Use MODE 1 for: “Put this in the Jesus TV frame,” “Frame this for Jesus TV,” “Use our standard Jesus TV frame,” or “JTV frame this.”

Use MODE 2 for: “Make #2 of 500,” “Give me #27 of 500,” “500 Scenes — number 83,” “Make the next Jesus TV 500 scene,” “Use our #1 of 500 look,” or “Do this in the 500 Scenes style.”

The Two Rules

NORMAL JESUS TV:
NEW PICTURE + SAME JESUS TV FRAME.
500 SCENES JESUS TV:
NEW NUMBER + SAME BLACK-AND-WHITE STATIC STYLE + SAME JESUS TV FRAME.
