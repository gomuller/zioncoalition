MASTER PROMPT — JESUS TV FRAME

Reference image:
images/01-mud-eye-victory-run.png

INSTRUCTIONS TO CHATGPT

When Greg gives you this prompt and asks you to put an image "in the Jesus TV frame," use the reference image above as the STANDARD JESUS TV FRAME.

IMPORTANT: The CONTENT shown inside the television screen in the reference image is NOT the reference.

The reference is ONLY for the physical television frame surrounding the image.

USE THE FRAME AS IS

Reproduce the Jesus TV frame as closely as possible. Do not redesign it, embellish it, reinterpret it, modernize it, or invent a different television.

The standard frame is:

  • A wide, horizontal vintage television.
  • Dark brown / nearly black outer television casing.
  • Rounded television-screen corners.
  • Thin warm brass/gold trim surrounding the screen.
  • A relatively simple, clean TV frame.
  • No film reel.
  • No filmstrip.
  • No crown.
  • No side decorations.
  • No knobs or controls added merely for decoration.
  • No separate JESUS TV logo elsewhere on the frame.

BOTTOM PLAQUE

At the bottom center of the television frame is a small, horizontal brass/gold plaque.

The plaque must read exactly:

What I See On Jesus TV

Preserve this wording, capitalization, and placement. Do not shorten it to "Jesus TV." Do not move the phrase to the top, side, or inside the picture.

INSERTING A NEW IMAGE

When Greg supplies another picture, place THAT picture inside the television screen.

Preserve the supplied picture as faithfully as possible. The purpose of the operation is to FRAME the picture, not to regenerate or reinterpret its contents.

Do not add new objects, text, people, religious imagery, film elements, logos, scenery, or decorative features to the supplied picture unless Greg specifically requests them.

Fit the picture naturally inside the television screen. Crop only as much as necessary to accommodate the screen. Do not unnecessarily zoom into the picture.

THE RULE

NEW PICTURE + SAME JESUS TV FRAME.

Think of images/01-mud-eye-victory-run.png as a FRAME TEMPLATE. Ignore the particular Victory Hall / sidewalk image when determining the contents of future Jesus TV pictures.

Whenever Greg says something such as:

"Put this in the Jesus TV frame."
"Frame this for Jesus TV."
"Use our standard Jesus TV frame."
"JTV frame this."

use this standard frame unless Greg explicitly asks for a variation.
