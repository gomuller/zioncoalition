50 Foundational Messages to Tomorrow's Greg

A living inheritance from today's Greg to tomorrow's Greg.

What are the 50 messages?

I'm identifying the fifty most foundational things I want tomorrow's Greg to remember, believe, practice, and become. These are not merely a list of religious propositions. Each is a direct message from today's Greg to tomorrow's Greg—a way of passing forward what I've learned, loved, and experimented with.

My Jesus Groundhog Day is made of deliberate SCENES—including the foundational Womb/Tomb waking scene. I want these messages to find expression in the conversations, songs, gestures, reminders, and acts of everyday life. Today's Greg can prepare a spiritual inheritance that tomorrow's Greg can actually use.

Eventually, I want to examine my writings, documents, and conversations across the years to discover the major recurring themes, rather than simply inventing fifty items. Some messages may turn out to be different expressions of a smaller number of foundational principles—like a few chords that make many songs possible.

The practices become the preaching. Each message will include a growing inventory of the ways my Jesus-Greg WORLD and Jesus Groundhog Day reinforce it through daily scenes, songs, gestures, objects, relationships, and actions. Read why reinforcement is essential to the Fifty Messages.

Working list: 6 of 50 identified so far. The order, wording, and connections are provisional and may change as the experiment develops.

MESSAGE #1

Jesus' New Name — My Best Friend, Savior, God, and King

Greg, remember Jesus' New Name:

“Jesus you're my best friend You're my Savior God and King”

Remember the great experiment of your life: What if Jesus really is your best friend?

Don't merely contemplate the possibility. Test it. Talk with Him. Sing to Him. Remember Him. Invite Him into your ordinary activities. Act as though your best friend is really here, and observe what happens to your life.

This is one of the names of Jesus in My Jesus-Greg WORLD, not a proposed universal or canonical title. It is the central hypothesis that gives the other practices their purpose.

SCENE connection: Every scene can become an opportunity to explore what living with Jesus as my best friend might mean.

MESSAGE #2

Prayer — Living in Deliberate Relationship with God

Greg, remember to talk with Heavenly Father and Jesus. Speak as though they are present, attentive, and consequential. Sing your love. Give thanks. Ask questions. Listen. Be willing to be wrought upon. And let that relationship shape how you rest, love, serve, and live.

Scriptural anchors: Alma 34:17–28; 2 Nephi 32:8–9; Romans 8:26–27. SCENE connection: Conversations, hymns, gratitude, listening, and discernment throughout the day.

MESSAGE #3

Scripture Study — Listening for God Through Sacred Writings

Greg, open the scriptures as though God might be speaking to you through them. Take the ancient witnesses seriously, even when their stories seem strange, mysterious, or difficult to believe. Remember that Nephi and others were recording their encounters with God. Read their stories, learn from their experiences, and allow yourself to be wrought upon. And remember, Greg: another story must begin! You are living and writing your own sacred history, one day at a time.

Scriptural anchors: 2 Nephi 32:3–5; 1 Nephi 19:23; 2 Nephi 29:11–12. SCENE connection: Ancient sacred stories becoming invitations to remember, interpret, and record the present.

MESSAGE #4

Jesus and I Are Doing Vicarious Work for the Living and the Dead

Message #4 those who are with us are more than we previously supposed (and those those are a multitude).

— 2 Kings 6:16, adapted

Remember the hosts of heaven—mortal and immortal—and the countless people who have knowingly or unknowingly prepared your inheritance, borne your burdens, and helped make your life possible.

Remember that Jesus and you are doing vicarious work for the living and the dead. Watch for His fingerprints throughout history and throughout your ordinary day.

Receive your inheritance with gratitude. Bear the burdens of others, including people you may never meet and generations yet unborn.

Maybe they'll thank you later.

Scriptural anchors: 2 Kings 6:16 (adapted); Mosiah 18:8–9; Malachi 4:5–6; Doctrine and Covenants 128:15. SCENE connection: Jesus TV, ancestor remembrance, the 500 favorite times, and recognition of other people's contributions.

Reinforcement — Eyes VeilKey: My eyes carry me to Victory Hall and the Nanomarathon, running with Jesus and friends; to the song Christ Now Reign; to Isaiah 40:31 and Doctrine and Covenants 89:20 (“run and not be weary”); and to 2 Kings 6:16–17, where opened eyes reveal that more are with us than we supposed. This reminds me to recognize the many people who help me, seen and unseen. Visit Eyes in the VeilKey inventory.

Read “You'll Thank Me Later”

MESSAGE #5

We Never Pray Alone — Greg + Jesus + the Holy Ghost

Greg, remember: From here on out, we never pray alone.

Whenever we pray to Heavenly Father, remember that we are approaching Him in group prayer:

Greg + Jesus + the Holy Ghost.

When you clasp your hands in prayer, remember that one hand represents Greg's hand, and the other represents the hand of God—Jesus, with the Holy Ghost.

Let this simple gesture remind you that prayer is not merely something you do toward God. In your sacred imagination, it is something you do with Jesus and the Holy Ghost.

Remember, Greg: We never pray alone.

Scriptural anchors: Romans 8:26–27; Hebrews 7:25; 3 Nephi 18:19–20; 2 Nephi 32:8–9. SCENE connection: Clasping hands in the Womb/Tomb waking scene and every subsequent prayer as a reminder of shared prayer and divine companionship.

This message remains distinct from #1 (Jesus as best friend) and #2 (the practice of prayer): #5 focuses on remembering companionship within prayer.

MESSAGE #6

Greg, Remember — You Are Called to Build Zion NOW, in the Open

Greg, remember: You are called to build Zion now. Build Zion in the open, like Noah built the ark in the open.

Don't merely wait for Zion to begin someday. In your Jesus-Greg WORLD, live and work as though the building has already begun. Zion Coalition is one of your experiments in that work.

Remember: Heaven Can't Wait — Born Again — Another Story Must Begin. Zion is a city of heaven brought to earth. Your story calls you to begin building, here and now.

Look for the unwitting builders. People may already have prepared pieces of Zion without knowing it: a song, a kindness, a useful invention, a burden quietly borne. I can receive what is lovely and praiseworthy, make its connection to Jesus more explicit in my own work, and honor its contributors without claiming they intended my interpretation. I need not build every piece from scratch.

Begin with prototypes, rough stones, imperfect first versions, and small public acts. There may be many revisions before any more mature form emerges. You need not wait for a finished plan before taking a faithful next step.

Brigham Young taught the centrality of building Zion to the Saints' work. Let his recurring emphasis — Zion is our business — remind you what business you are about.

And remember that this is your personal conviction and calling as you understand it, not a claim that other people must accept your interpretation or join your project.

Scriptural anchors: Moses 7:18; Doctrine and Covenants 6:6; Doctrine and Covenants 64:33; 1 Nephi 17–18 (Nephi building a ship); Genesis 6 (Noah building the ark). SCENE connection: The opera, the open construction of Zion Coalition, daily prototypes, and recognition letters.

Reinforcements — a growing inventory:

  1. Already in the WORLD: The name Zion Coalition repeatedly asks what I am building and why a coalition is needed.
  2. Already in the WORLD: My opera, Heaven Can't Wait — Born Again — Another Story Must Begin, reminds me that the work is underway now.
  3. Already in the WORLD: The public homepage, its evolving exhibits, and unfinished prototypes are acts of building in the open rather than waiting for perfection.
  4. Being developed: Letters recognizing people who have blessed my life, and symbolic Zion Coalition inductions, reinforce both Message #4 (recognize the multitude) and Message #6 (participate in building Zion). Induction into my artistic world does not imply a recipient's consent or endorsement.
  5. Being developed: Repeatedly asking during ordinary daily work, “What am I doing? Oh yes — building early Zion,” so the purpose becomes part of Jesus Groundhog Day.

Why the reinforcements matter
