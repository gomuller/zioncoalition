Zion Coalition • Sacred-Clown Thought Piece

Wait—Did I Sign Up for This?

Axl Rose, premortal paperwork, and the strange experience of waking up inside something much bigger than yourself.

Axl Rose, I understand your confusion.

You didn't apply for membership in Zion Coalition.

You didn't fill out a form.

You didn't click JOIN.

You just woke up one day and Greg Muller was informing you:

Congratulations, Axl. You're in.

That must seem terribly unfair.

Except...

Welcome to my world.

Literally.

Because one of the strangest things about my existence is that I seem to have been enrolled in an extraordinarily elaborate organization called:

EARTH

I woke up here.

Male.

Human.

American.

Born in 1963.

Gravity already activated.

Mortality enabled.

Family assigned.

Body issued.

Rules apparently already in effect.

Death clause included.

And I don't remember signing anything.

Did I sign up for this??

Latter-day Saint theology gives that question a particularly interesting twist because it teaches a premortal existence.

I existed before birth.

I participated in God's plan.

Coming to earth wasn't simply the beginning of Greg.

So perhaps somewhere in the forgotten premortal paperwork is my signature.

Maybe I really did click:

I AGREE.

Unfortunately, mortality apparently comes with a rather aggressive privacy policy:

You don't get to remember doing it.

Which makes my sacred-clown treatment of Axl considerably funnier to me.

“Come on, Axl. Don't complain to me about being mysteriously enrolled in something bigger than yourself.

Look where I woke up.”

And maybe that's another reason Zion Coalition feels familiar.

It is a tiny performance-art echo of the much larger situation in which I already find myself.

I have been enfolded into a matrix I did not construct.

I inherited its rules.

I inherited its history.

I inherited its people.

I inherited enormous amounts of completed work.

I inherited problems I didn't create and blessings I didn't earn.

I arrived inside a world already in motion.

And now Jesus seems to be saying:

Greg, you're here. Look around. Receive what has been prepared. Find your little piece. I'll show you what to do with it.

Which sounds suspiciously like what Zion Coalition tells Axl.

Maybe Axl's objection is mine:

“But I don't remember signing up!”

Exactly.

Neither do I.

Maybe I did.

Maybe somewhere in the premortal fine print I enthusiastically agreed to the whole thing.

Maybe mortality itself requires entering a world whose full terms I can no longer remember.

Either way, here I am.

And here you are.

Already inside something much larger than either of us built.

So, welcome to the club, brother Rose.

I'll send you the forms.
