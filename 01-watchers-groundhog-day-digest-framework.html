Editorial rules

Lead with lived change, not publication counts. Be candid about status: Imagined means proposed; Experimenting means actively trying; Inhabiting means recurring and established. Keep the front-stage explanation short. Do not confuse a published idea with a change in daily practice. Let the Watchers follow the development over successive months.
