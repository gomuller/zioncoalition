Yellow Muller Menu — Draft 1.0 Status

GitHub draft assembled November 14, 2026. This is a family restaurant / performance-art concept, not a claim of public dining service.

Completed

  • Seven-day menu cards with recipe links for all listed main-dish alternatives
  • 17 newly added recipe pages: 8 archived-source working drafts and 9 provisional trials
  • Existing masala, General Tso’s, pizza and pita recipes preserved
  • Separate soup and curry collection and side-dish collection
  • Ingredient requirements for 17 newly created recipes captured in structured shopping data
  • Weekly kitchen planner for selecting one dish per day and viewing ingredient requirements
  • Questions for Greg review queue

Not finished

  • Nine trial recipes need actual family formulas or cooking tests, especially Grammy Sherry’s noodles
  • Shared ingredients are not yet mathematically normalized or totaled into a reliable shopping purchase order
  • Inventory, quantities for household size, vendor pricing, and food-cost calculations are unverified
  • Live deployed-site navigation and appearance have not been comprehensively tested
  • Back-of-house pages are publicly accessible by URL; no access control is implemented
  • Some source recipes contain ambiguous instructions or quantities needing family verification

Next review

Test each day’s dinner and record any adjustments in Questions for Greg. Verify the live website and then choose whether the kitchen area should be access-controlled.
