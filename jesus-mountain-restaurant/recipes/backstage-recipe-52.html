Pancakes

External-source-inspired testable prototype — not the historical family recipe. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

1½ cups flour; 2 tsp baking powder; ¾ tsp salt; 2 tbsp sugar; 2 eggs; 1¼ cups milk; 3 tbsp melted butter.

Method

Beat eggs and milk until foamy. Stir in butter; fold in combined dry ingredients. Rest 15 minutes. Cook ¼-cup portions on lightly greased medium griddle until bubbles form; flip once and brown.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
