Black Beluga Lentil Soup

Recovered family archive — working transcription. Not yet retested or approved for the current restaurant menu. Family source preserved below.

Ingredients

2 cups black beluga lentils; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 large chopped onion; 1 tsp salt; one 28-oz can crushed tomatoes; 2 cups water; 3 cups chopped chard or kale.

Method

Cook rinsed lentils in 6 cups boiling water about 20 minutes until tender, then drain. Sauté onion with salt in oil until tender. Add tomatoes, lentils and 2 cups water; simmer briefly. Stir in chopped greens until wilted. Adjust seasoning; optional smoked paprika, chiles or cumin.
