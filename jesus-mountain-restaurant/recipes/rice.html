Muller Rice

Shared staple · Recovered family source — working draft

Shared across General Tso’s, burritos and other dishes. Rice storage: promptly refrigerate cooked rice in shallow containers; do not leave at room temperature.

Ingredients

1 cup rice; 1½ cups water; optional butter, salt, pepper.

How to prepare

Bring rice and water to a boil. Cover and cook on low for approximately 15–20 minutes until rice is tender and liquid absorbed. Rest covered briefly.
