Sammy Jammy’s Chocolate-Chip Oat Cookies

Recovered family archive — working transcription. Not yet retested or approved for the current restaurant menu. Family source preserved below.

Ingredients

2 cups whole-wheat flour; 1 tsp baking powder; ½ tsp baking soda; ¼ tsp salt; 2 cups packed brown sugar; 1 cup butter; 2 cups rolled oats; 2 eggs; 1 tbsp milk; 12 oz semisweet chocolate chips.

Method

Heat oven to 350°F. Mix flour, baking powder, soda and salt. Beat sugar and butter, then add oats, eggs and milk. Mix in dry ingredients and chocolate chips. Scoop ¼-cup portions 3 inches apart on greased sheets. Bake 12–13 minutes, until edges begin to brown; cool 2 minutes on sheets before transferring to racks.
