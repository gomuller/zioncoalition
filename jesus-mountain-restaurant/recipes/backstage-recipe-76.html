Caesar Dressing

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

2½ cups mayonnaise; ⅓ cup lemon juice; 1 cup grated Parmesan; 6 garlic cloves; 2 tsp Worcestershire; 4 tsp Dijon mustard; ½ tsp black pepper.

Method

Blend all ingredients 30 seconds until smooth. Chill before use.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
