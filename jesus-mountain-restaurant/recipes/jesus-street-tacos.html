Jesus Street Tacos — Trial

Wednesday · Provisional trial recipe — NOT verified family original

Provisional family trial based on the restaurant concept, not a recovered historical recipe. Meat must be cooked safely.

Ingredients

12 small tortillas; 1 lb cooked shredded beef or lamb; ½ onion finely diced; cilantro; lime; pico de gallo or Saebra’s salsa; optional cabbage.

How to prepare

Warm cooked meat until thoroughly hot, season to taste. Warm tortillas and assemble with onion, cilantro, lime and salsa.
