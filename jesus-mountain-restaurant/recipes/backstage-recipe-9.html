Cowboy Casserole

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; family casserole unspecified. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

1 lb ground beef; 1 chopped onion; 1 can kidney beans; 1 can diced tomatoes; 1 cup corn; 2 cups cooked pasta; 1 cup cheese; 1 tsp chili powder.

Method

Heat oven to 375°F. Brown beef and onion; drain. Combine with beans, tomatoes, corn, pasta and chili powder. Top with cheese; bake 20 minutes until hot.
