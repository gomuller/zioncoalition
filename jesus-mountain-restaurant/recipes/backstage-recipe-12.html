Lasagna

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; family lasagna unspecified. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

12 lasagna noodles; 1 lb ground beef; 24 oz tomato pasta sauce; 15 oz ricotta; 1 egg; 2 cups mozzarella; ½ cup Parmesan.

Method

Brown beef, stir in sauce. Mix ricotta and egg. Layer sauce, noodles, ricotta and mozzarella three times; finish with Parmesan. Cover, bake at 375°F 40 minutes, uncover 15; rest 15.
