Muller Smothered Burritos

Wednesday · Recovered family source — working draft

Source contains two versions (large 28-burrito freezer batch and smaller red-sauce bake); instructions are combined only as a working adaptation, not a verified exact original process.

Ingredients

Batch makes 28 burritos: 28 tortillas; tortilla dough 6 cups flour, 2 cups water, ½ cup oil, 4 tablespoons sugar, 1 tablespoon salt. Filling: 4 cans drained beans, 2 cups sauce, 1 pound cheese, diced onion, pepper and carrots. Sauce: 16 cups broth, roux from 8 oz butter or oil plus 1 cup flour; chili powder, salt, garlic, cumin, oregano, about 1 cup each ketchup and minced onions to taste.

How to prepare

Mix tortilla dough, divide and roll thin; cook tortillas in a hot skillet. Cook vegetables and mix filling with beans, cheese and some sauce. Whisk sauce ingredients and simmer until thick. Fill tortillas, roll, and coat with sauce. Bake at 375°F, covered 20 minutes and uncovered 10 minutes according to the smaller alternate family recipe. Serve with lettuce, tomatoes and yogurt.
