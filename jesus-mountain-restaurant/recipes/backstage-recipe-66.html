Cowboy Caviar

External-source-inspired testable prototype — not the historical family recipe. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

One 15-oz can black beans; one 15-oz can black-eyed peas; 1 diced bell pepper; ½ diced red onion; 1 diced tomato; ¼ cup chopped cilantro. Dressing: 2 tbsp olive oil; juice of 1 lime; ½ tbsp balsamic vinegar; ½ tsp chili powder; 1 tsp cumin; ½ tsp salt; ½ tsp sugar.

Method

Rinse beans and peas. Dice vegetables. Whisk dressing separately, toss with vegetables and beans. Chill 20 minutes and serve as salad or dip.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
