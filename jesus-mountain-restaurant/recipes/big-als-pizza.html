Big Al’s Pepperoni Pizza

Friday dinner · Historical Muller family recipe · Working draft

Original recipe does not specify can size, cheese quantity, pepperoni quantity, dough formula or pizza yield. These are deliberately left open rather than invented.

Ingredients

IngredientQuantity in family source
Risen whole-wheat bread dough¼ batch; underlying dough recipe pending
Stewed tomatoes1 can (source abbreviation: 1CN)
Garlic1 teaspoon, minced
Basil1 tablespoon
Mozzarella or other cheeseTo taste; quantity not specified
PepperoniTo taste; quantity not specified
Red vinegarOptional 1 tablespoon
ThymeOptional ½ teaspoon
OreganoOptional ½ teaspoon
Hot pepper flakes and black pepperOptional, to taste

Preparation

  1. Prepare and rise a quarter-batch of the family whole-wheat bread dough (formula still to be recovered).
  2. Mash stewed tomatoes with a whisk to the desired consistency; mix with garlic, basil and any optional sauce ingredients.
  3. Shape the dough, spread with sauce, add cheese and pepperoni.
  4. Bake at 450°F for about 10–12 minutes, adjusting for thickness and toppings; serve piping hot.

Preparation, storage and kits

The family source identifies pizza as a foundation for cheese pizza, pepperoni rolls, stromboli, focaccia and other bread forms. Dough and sauce can be organized as separate preparation kits; exact freezing procedure remains to be family-tested.

Why we buy and prepare it this way

Big Al’s pizza is a family touchstone dedicated to Alan Muller. The original text connects his enthusiasm for markets, chefs, nutrition and repeated recipe refinement with the larger family food system.

Store perishable ingredients promptly at 40°F or below and frozen items at 0°F or below. Refrigerate prepared foods within two hours. These are general food-safety guidelines, not claims from the historical recipe.
