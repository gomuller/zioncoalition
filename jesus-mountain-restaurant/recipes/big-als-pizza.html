Big Al’s Pepperoni Pizza

Friday · Historical Muller family recipe · Awaiting family verification

The source assumes an existing whole-wheat bread-dough recipe. It also contains family stories about Alan Muller and pizza variations.

Ingredients

¼ batch risen whole-wheat bread dough; 1 can stewed tomatoes; 1 tsp minced garlic; 1 tbsp basil; cheese and pepperoni. Optional sauce: 1 tbsp red vinegar, ½ tsp thyme, ½ tsp oregano, hot pepper flakes and black pepper to taste.

Method

Mash stewed tomatoes to desired consistency; combine with garlic, basil and any optional seasonings. Spread risen dough, add sauce, cheese and pepperoni. Bake at 450°F for approximately 10–12 minutes, depending on toppings.
