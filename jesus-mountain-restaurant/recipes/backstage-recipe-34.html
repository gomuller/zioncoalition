Split Pea Soup / Soup Mix

Recovered family archive — working transcription. Not yet retested or approved for the current restaurant menu. Family source preserved below.

Ingredients

2 cups split peas or dried soup mix; 8–10 cups water; ½ cup chopped carrots; ½ cup chopped celery; ½ cup chopped onion; a 2-inch cube of salt pork or a ham bone.

Method

Combine ingredients, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook 45–60 minutes, or until peas are fully tender. Add salt to taste at the end.
