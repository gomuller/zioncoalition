HimChi

Signature everyday side · Kimchi with a crisp apple wedge

Jesus is the Giver of Life. A little red bowl at the family table every day—something familiar and life-affirming alongside seven days of different dinners. Our name is HimChi, with a big H and a big C. The apple is part of the presentation, not an afterthought.

Kitchen trial recipe — vegetarian, about 1 quart

This is a proposed house recipe to test and refine, not a recovered Muller family formula. For the daily version, prepare kimchi in batches and add fresh apple at serving time.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium napa cabbage (about 2 lb / 900 g), chopped
  • 18 g non-iodized salt (2% of cabbage weight), plus enough water if needed to keep the cabbage submerged
  • 4 scallions, sliced
  • 1 medium carrot, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh grated ginger
  • 2–3 tablespoons Korean gochugaru chili flakes, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon sugar, optional
  • 1–2 teaspoons white or yellow miso, optional, for savory depth (check ingredients for vegetarian suitability)
  • Fresh crisp red apple: 1–2 thin slices or a small wedge per bowl, added just before eating
  • Optional sesame seeds for garnish

Preparation

  1. Wash hands and utensils. Trim cabbage, weigh it, and mix with 2% salt by weight. Massage and rest 1–2 hours until softened and juicy.
  2. Mix garlic, ginger, gochugaru, optional sugar and miso. Fold into cabbage with carrot and scallions.
  3. Pack tightly into a clean fermentation jar, pressing vegetables under their liquid. If needed, add a 2% saltwater brine. Leave ample headspace; use a fermentation lid or vent safely as directed by your jar maker.
  4. Ferment at a moderate room temperature (roughly 65–72°F) for about 1–3 days, checking daily for a pleasantly sour aroma and bubbling. Keep vegetables submerged. Refrigerate once tangy; flavor continues to develop slowly in the fridge.
  5. Serve a small chilled portion in a clear glass bowl with fresh red apple slices tucked alongside. Add scallions or sesame seeds if desired.

Food safety: Use clean equipment, keep vegetables under brine, refrigerate the finished kimchi at 40°F or colder, and discard if moldy or putrid. For reliable fermentation safety, use a tested fermentation recipe and check that the finished product reaches pH 4.6 or below; if uncertain, choose refrigerated commercially prepared kimchi instead. Fermented food is not automatically probiotic or medically therapeutic.

Backstage routine

Make ahead: A batch of kimchi for the week; keep refrigerated. Daily front stage: A small glass bowl with a fresh apple wedge or slices. Menu organization: HimChi is a standing daily side, not one of the seven rotating main meals. Guests can enjoy it with any day's dinner.
