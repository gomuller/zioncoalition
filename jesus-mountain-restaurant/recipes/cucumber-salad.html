Garlic-Sesame Cucumber Salad

Working recipe · approximately four servings · family testing invited

Ingredients

2 cucumbers; 1 tbsp rice vinegar; 1 tsp soy sauce; ½ tsp sesame oil; 1 minced garlic clove; optional sesame seeds

Method

Slice or smash cucumbers. Mix vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil and garlic. Toss with cucumbers and chill 10–15 minutes. Add sesame seeds if desired.

Shopping and storage logic

Buy fresh cucumbers as needed; dress close to serving.
