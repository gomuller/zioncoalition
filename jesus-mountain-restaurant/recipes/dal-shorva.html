Dal Shorva Lentil Soup

Tuesday / Soups · Recovered family source — working draft

The archived method mentions tomatoes twice; use the single listed can. The same Drive file also contains a larger Dal Shorva 2 freezer-batch variation.

Ingredients

1 cup red lentils; 4 cups chicken broth or water; 1 can (14 oz) stewed tomatoes; ¼ cup milk powder; ½ tsp turmeric; 1-inch ginger; 1½ tsp salt; ¼ cup butter; 1 yellow onion; 1 tsp cumin seeds; black pepper; optional ¼ cup cilantro.

How to prepare

Rinse lentils. Simmer with broth, turmeric, ginger and tomatoes about 25 minutes until tender. Purée if desired. Stir in milk powder and salt and heat through. Brown onion with butter and cumin; add as topping with pepper and cilantro.
