Mukhwas — Sweet Fennel

Recovered family archive — working transcription. Not yet retested or approved for the current restaurant menu. Family source preserved below.

Ingredients

3 cups fennel seeds; ¼ cup sugar; ¼ cup water; cinnamon to taste.

Method

Combine 2 cups fennel seeds with sugar and water; stir constantly over medium-high heat about 8 minutes, until the liquid cooks away and seeds are coated. Cool, then stir in the remaining cup of uncooked fennel seeds. Store airtight.
