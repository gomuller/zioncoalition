Naan — Trial Flatbread

Monday / Tuesday · Provisional trial recipe — NOT verified family original

Provisional draft, not a recovered Muller naan recipe. Test and revise.

Ingredients

2 cups all-purpose flour; ¾ cup plain yogurt; ½ tsp salt; 1 tsp baking powder; 2–5 tbsp water as needed; oil for skillet.

How to prepare

Mix flour, salt and baking powder; stir in yogurt and enough water for a soft dough. Rest 15 minutes, divide into 6 pieces, roll thin and cook in a lightly oiled hot skillet about 1–2 minutes per side until cooked.
