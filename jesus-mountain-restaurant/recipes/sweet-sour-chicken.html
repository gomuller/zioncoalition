Sweet & Sour Chicken — Trial

Thursday · Provisional trial recipe — NOT verified family original

Provisional draft. Distinct from the confirmed General Tso’s sauce. Reheat chicken thoroughly.

Ingredients

1 lb cooked crispy chicken; ⅓ cup ketchup; ¼ cup vinegar; ⅓ cup sugar; ½ cup water; 1 tbsp soy sauce; 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water; optional pineapple chunks and peppers.

How to prepare

Bring ketchup, vinegar, sugar, water and soy sauce to a simmer. Add cornstarch slurry, stir until thickened. Toss with hot chicken just before serving; add pineapple or peppers if desired.
