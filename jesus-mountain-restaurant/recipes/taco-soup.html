Taco Soup

TESTABLE PROTOTYPE — not an authenticated Muller family recipe. Source research: original recipe / technique reference. Adapted and summarized from a Budget Bytes kitchen-tested recipe; family version not recovered.

Yield and time: 6 servings; about 40 minutes

Ingredients

1 tbsp olive oil; 1 lb ground beef; 1 diced onion; 4 minced garlic cloves; 1 cup frozen corn; two 10-oz cans diced tomatoes with green chiles; one 15-oz can pinto beans, drained; one 15-oz can black beans, drained; 1 oz taco seasoning; 1 oz ranch seasoning; 3 tbsp tomato paste; 2 cups water. Optional cheese, cilantro, lime, sour cream.

Method

Brown beef in oil and drain excess fat if needed. Add onion and garlic and soften. Stir in corn, beans and tomatoes with juices. Add tomato paste, both seasoning mixes and water. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered about 15 minutes. Taste before adding salt; serve with toppings.
