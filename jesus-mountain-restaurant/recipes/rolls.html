Dinner Rolls from Mother Dough — Trial

Tuesday · Provisional trial recipe — NOT verified family original

Adaptation from the recovered whole-wheat bread dough; roll time and yield have not been family-tested.

Ingredients

¼ batch risen Muller whole-wheat bread dough; optional butter for finishing.

How to prepare

After the first rise, divide into small rolls, place on a greased baking sheet, allow to rise until puffy, then bake at 400°F until browned and cooked through (start checking around 15 minutes).
