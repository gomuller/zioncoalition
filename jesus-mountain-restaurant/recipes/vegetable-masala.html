Vegetable Masala

Monday dinner · Historical Muller family recipe · Working draft

This is the first of two different masala formulas preserved in the Drive document. The second contains chickpeas, peas and coconut milk; we have not merged them. The original says “1 inch carrot,” which needs clarification. Yield not specified.

CarrotSource says “1 inch carrot” — clarify
Potato½ cup

Yogurt½ cup

Preparation

  1. Heat the oil. Add mustard, cumin and coriander seeds; stir until popping, without burning.
  2. Add chopped Anaheim pepper, garlic, ginger and onion; cook until onion is translucent.
  3. Stir in turmeric, garam masala, salt, sugar and red pepper flakes; cook a couple of minutes.
  4. Add stewed tomatoes, yogurt and vegetables. Simmer gently until potatoes and carrots are tender.
  5. Serve with naan if available; the naan formula is still to be recovered.

Preparation, storage and kits

Whole spices can be measured into a dry spice kit ahead of time. Chop vegetables shortly before cooking, or prep and refrigerate safely. Yogurt stays refrigerated. No family-tested freezer protocol yet.

Why we buy and prepare it this way

Keep whole mustard, cumin and coriander seeds on the shopping list as specified in the historical recipe; record your preferred supplier and why later.

Store perishable ingredients promptly at 40°F or below and frozen items at 0°F or below. Refrigerate prepared foods within two hours. These are general food-safety guidelines, not claims from the historical recipe.
