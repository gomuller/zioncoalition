Vegetable Masala

Monday · Historical Muller family recipe · Awaiting family verification

undefined

Ingredients

1 tbsp oil; 1 tsp black mustard seeds; 1 tsp cumin seeds; ½ tsp coriander seeds; ½ Anaheim pepper; 2 garlic cloves; 2 tsp fresh ginger; ½ onion; ½ tsp turmeric; ½ tsp garam masala; ½ tsp salt; ½ tsp sugar; pinch red pepper flakes; about 1 inch carrot, ½ cup potato, ½ cup cauliflower; ½ can (14 oz) stewed tomatoes; ½ cup yogurt.

Method

Heat oil; toast mustard, cumin and coriander seeds until they pop without burning. Add pepper, garlic, ginger and onion; cook until translucent. Add remaining spices, salt and sugar. After a couple of minutes, add tomatoes, yogurt and vegetables. Simmer on low until potato and carrot are tender.
