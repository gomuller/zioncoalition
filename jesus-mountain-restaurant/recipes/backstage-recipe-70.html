Cucumber-Mint Raita

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

1 large cucumber, grated and squeezed dry; 2 cups plain whole-milk yogurt; 10 mint leaves sliced; ½ tsp cumin; pinch cayenne; pinch paprika; salt and pepper.

Method

Combine all ingredients; chill before serving.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
