Cabbage Tacos

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; family recipe not located. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

8 tortillas; 3 cups finely shredded cabbage; 1 cup cooked beans; ½ cup salsa; ½ cup yogurt; lime.

Method

Warm beans and tortillas. Toss cabbage with lime and salt; fill tortillas with beans, cabbage, salsa and yogurt.

Archive and testing

Family sources: Greg Recipes, Food Notes, Food Room. For non-family prototypes, an independently tested published source still needs verification; do not treat this as a sourced external recipe.
