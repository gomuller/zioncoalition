Family Bean Soup

Recovered family archive — working transcription. Not yet retested or approved for the current restaurant menu. Family source preserved below.

Ingredients

1 lb assorted dried beans; one 32-oz can tomatoes; three 14-oz cans chicken broth; 2 cups water; 1 cup white wine; 1½ cups chopped onion; 4 sliced carrots; 3 sliced celery stalks; ½ tsp garlic powder; 1 tbsp pepper; 2 tsp paprika; 2 tsp parsley; 2 cups diced ham.

Method

Soak beans overnight, drain and rinse. Combine all ingredients in a slow cooker and cook 6–8 hours on high or all day on low, until beans are tender; alternatively simmer on stovetop 4–6 hours. Check bean doneness and season to taste.
