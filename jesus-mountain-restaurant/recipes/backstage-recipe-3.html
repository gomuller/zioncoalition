Freebird Burritos

Family archive-derived working draft. family notes: Freebird burritos use taco ingredients including rice. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

4 tortillas; 2 cups cooked rice; 2 cups seasoned beans; 1 cup shredded cheese; 1 cup salsa; lettuce.

Method

Warm tortillas and filling. Divide rice, beans, cheese, salsa and lettuce among tortillas; roll and serve.

Archive and testing

Family sources: Greg Recipes, Food Notes, Food Room. For non-family prototypes, an independently tested published source still needs verification; do not treat this as a sourced external recipe.
