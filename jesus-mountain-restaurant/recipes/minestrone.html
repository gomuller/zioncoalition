Minestrone

Recipe status: Recovered family archive recipe.

Ingredients

1 oz butter; 1 tbsp olive oil; ½ lb carrots; ¼ lb tomatoes; ¼ lb celery; ¼ lb onion; 1 tsp garlic; ¼ lb green beans; 1 lb savoy cabbage; ¼ lb zucchini; 1 tsp parsley; 1 tsp basil; 1 gallon chicken stock; 1 bay leaf; 1–2 oz prosciutto; 2 oz Romano cheese rind; 1 lb potatoes; 1 can each kidney beans, garbanzo beans and cannellini beans (15 oz each); 2 oz grated Romano; salt and pepper.

Directions / next action

Heat butter and oil; sauté celery, onion, carrots and garlic until soft. Add green beans, cabbage, zucchini, parsley and basil and cook until soft. Add stock, bay leaf, tomatoes, prosciutto, Romano rind and potatoes. Bring briefly to a boil, then simmer 20–30 minutes until potatoes soften. Add canned beans with their liquid and simmer 5 minutes more. Remove rind and prosciutto, then stir in grated Romano. Archive directions preserved; test and adjust as needed.
