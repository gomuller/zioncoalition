Chicken Dumpling / Wonton Soup

TESTABLE PROTOTYPE — not an authenticated Muller family recipe. Source research: original recipe / technique reference. This is a chicken wonton-style prototype. The handwritten 'dumplings' could have meant a different dumpling tradition; family approval needed.

Yield and time: 4 bowls plus extra frozen dumplings; approximately 90 minutes

Ingredients

For dumplings: 1 lb ground chicken; ½ cup chopped parsley; 1 minced garlic clove; ¼ cup water; 2 tbsp dry sherry (optional); juice of ½ lemon; 1 tbsp oil; 1 tsp salt; 1 tsp sugar; ½ tsp thyme; ½ tsp pepper; 2 tbsp melted butter; 1 package wonton wrappers. For soup: 1 tbsp oil; ½ diced onion; 3 diced carrots; 3 diced celery stalks; 5 cups chicken broth; salt and pepper.

Method

Mix filling thoroughly until sticky. Place 1–2 tsp in each wrapper, moisten edges and seal. Freeze unused dumplings on a tray before bagging. For broth, sauté onion, carrots and celery in oil, add stock and simmer 20 minutes. Boil dumplings separately until fully cooked, approximately 5–6 minutes fresh or 7–8 frozen; ground chicken filling must reach 165°F. Divide among bowls and pour over hot broth.
