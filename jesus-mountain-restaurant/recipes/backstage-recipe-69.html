Raita

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

2 cups whole-milk yogurt; 1 seeded shredded cucumber; ¼ cup chopped red onion; ¼ cup cilantro; juice of 1 lemon; 1 tsp salt; pinch cumin; ⅛ tsp pepper; grated carrot, ginger and garlic to taste.

Method

Squeeze excess liquid from cucumber, combine all ingredients and chill 30 minutes. Taste for salt and acid.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
