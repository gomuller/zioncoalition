Matar Paneer

Family archive-derived working draft. family Drive notes; simplified working draft. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

2 cups peas; 1 cup cubed paneer; 1 onion; 2 garlic cloves; 1 tsp ginger; 1 can tomatoes; 1 tsp cumin; ½ tsp coriander; ½ tsp garam masala; ¼ tsp turmeric; 2 tbsp oil.

Method

Sauté onion in oil; add garlic, ginger and spices. Add tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes. Add peas and paneer; simmer until peas tender and cheese warmed.
