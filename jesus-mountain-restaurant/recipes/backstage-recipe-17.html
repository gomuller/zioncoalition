Pickle-Cheese Sandwiches

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; family sandwich unspecified. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

8 slices bread; 4 oz cheddar cheese; 8 pickle slices; 2 tbsp mayonnaise or mustard.

Method

Layer cheese and pickles on bread with spread; serve cold or grill until cheese melts.
