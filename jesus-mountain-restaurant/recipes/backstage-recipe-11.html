Pot Roast

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; family method unspecified. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

3 lb beef chuck roast; 1½ tsp salt; 1 tsp pepper; 2 tbsp oil; 1 onion; 4 carrots; 4 potatoes; 2 cups beef broth.

Method

Sear seasoned roast in oil. Put in covered Dutch oven with onion and broth; cook at 300°F 3 hours, add vegetables and cook 1–2 hours more until fork-tender.
