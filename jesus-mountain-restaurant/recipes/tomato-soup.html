Roasted Tomato Soup

TESTABLE PROTOTYPE — not an authenticated Muller family recipe. Source research: original recipe / technique reference. Original trial formulation inspired by the Budget Bytes roasted-tomato method, NOT a transcription of its exact ingredient quantities. Confirm after family taste test.

Yield and time: Prototype; quantities and method adapted for a family trial

Ingredients

2 lb ripe tomatoes, quartered; 1 medium onion, quartered; 5 garlic cloves, peeled; 2 tbsp olive oil; 2 cups vegetable or chicken broth; ½ tsp salt, plus more to taste; black pepper; 1 tsp dried basil or a small handful of fresh basil; optional 1 tsp balsamic vinegar.

Method

Heat oven to 425°F. Toss tomatoes, onion and garlic with oil and roast on a sheet pan 30–40 minutes, until browned at the edges. Transfer carefully to a pot with broth and basil, simmer 10 minutes, then blend until smooth. Season with salt, pepper and optional vinegar. Add broth to adjust consistency.
