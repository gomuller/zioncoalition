Pad Thai

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; not family Pad Thai. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

8 oz rice noodles; 2 eggs; 1 cup tofu or cooked chicken; 2 cups bean sprouts; 3 scallions; 2 tbsp oil; 3 tbsp fish sauce; 2 tbsp tamarind paste; 2 tbsp sugar; peanuts and lime.

Method

Soak noodles per package. Mix sauce ingredients. Scramble eggs and set aside. Stir-fry protein, noodles and sauce, adding water if needed; toss in sprouts, scallions and eggs. Top with peanuts and lime.

Archive and testing

Family sources: Greg Recipes, Food Notes, Food Room. For non-family prototypes, an independently tested published source still needs verification; do not treat this as a sourced external recipe.
