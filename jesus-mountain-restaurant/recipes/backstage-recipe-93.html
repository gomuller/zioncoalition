Family Cocoa

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

1 tbsp cocoa powder; 2 tbsp powdered milk; 1 tsp sugar; hot water to taste.

Method

Combine dry ingredients, whisk in a small amount of hot water to form a paste, then top up with hot water or milk.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
