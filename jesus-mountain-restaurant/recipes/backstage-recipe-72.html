Sweet Tamarind Chutney

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

½ cup tamarind; ¾ cup jaggery or sugar; 1 tsp red chili powder; 1 tsp cumin; 1½ cups water; ¼ tsp black salt; additional salt.

Method

Simmer tamarind in water 8–10 minutes. Mash and strain pulp. Return to saucepan, dissolve jaggery, add spices and simmer until syrupy. Cool and refrigerate.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
