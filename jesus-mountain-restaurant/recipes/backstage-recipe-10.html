Mexican Casserole

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; family casserole unspecified. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

8 corn tortillas; 2 cups cooked chicken or beans; 2 cups enchilada sauce; 1 cup corn; 1½ cups cheese; ½ diced onion.

Method

Heat oven to 375°F. Layer sauce, tortillas, filling, corn, onion and cheese in greased dish; repeat. Cover and bake 25 minutes; uncover for 10.
