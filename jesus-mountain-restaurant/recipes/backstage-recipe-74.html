Italian Dressing

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

6 tbsp olive oil; 2 tbsp white wine vinegar; 1 tbsp lemon juice; 2 minced garlic cloves; ⅛ tsp salt; ⅛ tsp pepper; 1 tsp basil; 1 tsp parsley; ½ tsp oregano; 2 tsp Parmesan.

Method

Whisk all ingredients. Refrigerate and shake before serving.
