Mango Chutney

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

2 cups sugar; 1 cup white vinegar; 6 cups diced mango; 1 diced onion; ½ cup golden raisins; ¼ cup minced crystallized ginger; 1 minced garlic clove; 1 tsp mustard seeds; ¼ tsp red pepper flakes.

Method

Boil sugar and vinegar until dissolved. Add other ingredients; simmer uncovered 45–60 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick. Refrigerate; use a tested canning procedure if preserving shelf-stable.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
