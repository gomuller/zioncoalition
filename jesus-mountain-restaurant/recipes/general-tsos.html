General Tso’s Chicken or Cauliflower

Thursday dinner · Muller family recipe · Working draft

The original sauce is established family practice. The chicken is batch-fried and frozen in individual bags. The cauliflower method is a first trial, to be tested by the family. This page is a recipe draft, not a claim of restaurant service.

Chicken: batch-prep and freeze

Working coating from our earlier kitchen discussion, for 5 lb boneless chicken: 2 cups cornstarch, 1 cup all-purpose flour, 4 eggs, ¼ cup soy sauce, 2 teaspoons garlic powder, 2 teaspoons ground ginger and 1 teaspoon black pepper; frying oil as needed.

  1. Cut chicken into bite-size pieces. Mix with eggs, soy sauce and seasonings; refrigerate 20–30 minutes.
  2. Combine flour and cornstarch; coat chicken thoroughly.
  3. Deep-fry in batches at about 350°F until golden and chicken reaches 165°F internally.
  4. Cool promptly, refrigerate or freeze within two hours, then freeze separately in individual portions. Do not sauce before freezing.
  5. For Thursday, reheat thoroughly to 165°F and toss with the hot sauce immediately before serving.

Exact fry/reheat times depend on piece size and equipment; use a food thermometer. This coating is a working transcription pending a final comparison with the original conversation.

Cauliflower: first test batch

Use approximately 1 lb cauliflower florets, 2 eggs, ¾ cup cornstarch, a pinch of salt and pepper, and frying oil. This straightforward coating follows the egg-and-cornstarch approach used in published General Tso’s cauliflower recipes.

  1. If using frozen cauliflower, thaw in the refrigerator just enough to separate florets; drain and pat thoroughly dry. Fresh florets are also suitable.
  2. Beat eggs with salt and pepper. Toss florets in egg, then add cornstarch gradually until evenly coated.
  3. Deep-fry in small batches at about 350°F until the coating is crisp and cauliflower is tender, generally around 5–6 minutes, depending on size and moisture. Drain on a rack.
  4. For this first trial, serve freshly fried with some of the same Muller sauce. If successful, test freezing and reheating a later batch.

Kitchen note: Prepare the cauliflower separately from raw chicken and keep the coatings and utensils separate.

Coating-method references: Food Network's General Tso's Cauliflower and The Kitchn's overview. We are not replacing the Muller sauce with theirs.

Rice and broccoli

The archived Muller recipe specifies 1 cup rice and 1½ cups water: bring to a boil, cover, and cook on low 15–20 minutes. Serve with broccoli florets. The original recipe suggests microwaving broccoli for about 3 minutes, adjusting until tender.
