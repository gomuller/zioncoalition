Pico de Gallo

Sides / Tacos · Recovered family source — working draft

Spelling standardized for navigation; quantities from Greg recipes.

Ingredients

3 tomatoes diced; ½ onion diced; 1 tbsp cilantro minced; 1 tsp minced jalapeño; ¼ tsp salt; ½ tbsp fresh lime or lemon juice.

How to prepare

Dice and mix all ingredients. Chill until serving.
