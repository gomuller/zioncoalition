Muller Whole-Wheat Bread / Mother Dough

Bread and dough · Recovered family source — working draft

The family recipe gives a loaf-baking method. Pizza uses ¼ of the risen batch; pitas use ½. Oven proofing must be warm, not hot enough to damage yeast.

Ingredients

1 quart wheat berries (about 6 cups flour); 2½ cups water; 4 tablespoons sugar; 1 tablespoon yeast; 1 tablespoon salt.

How to prepare

Dissolve yeast in water. Grind wheat berries. Mix all ingredients vigorously for 6 minutes. Oil loaf pans; place shaped loaves inside. Rise about 30 minutes in a switched-off, previously warmed oven. Remove pans, preheat to 400°F, bake about 23 minutes; butter tops after baking.
