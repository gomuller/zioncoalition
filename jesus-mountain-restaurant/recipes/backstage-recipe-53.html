Waffles

External-source-inspired testable prototype — not the historical family recipe. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

1 cup milk; 2 eggs; 1 tbsp vanilla; 5 tbsp melted butter; 2 cups self-rising flour; 2 tbsp sugar.

Method

Preheat waffle iron. Whisk milk, eggs, vanilla and butter; stir in flour and sugar just until combined. Cook according to waffle iron directions.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
