Chicken Cheese Broccoli — Trial

Thursday · Provisional trial recipe — NOT verified family original

Provisional draft; actual family formula not yet verified.

Ingredients

2 cups cooked chicken; 1 lb frozen broccoli; 1 cup shredded cheddar; ½ cup milk; 1 tbsp butter; 1 tbsp flour; salt and pepper; optional cooked rice.

How to prepare

Cook broccoli until hot. Make a sauce by melting butter, stirring in flour, then gradually whisking in milk until thick. Melt in cheese; fold in cooked chicken and broccoli. Serve over rice if desired.
