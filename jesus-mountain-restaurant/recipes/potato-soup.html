Potato & Cauliflower Soup

Recipe status: Recovered family archive recipe (file titled potato soup - clam chowder; no clams in recipe).

Ingredients

2 large baking potatoes, peeled and sliced; 3 cups milk; salt and pepper; ½ head cauliflower, chopped; 1 bunch scallions, whites and greens separated; 4 slices bacon; 1 cup shredded cheddar; ¼ cup sour cream.

Directions / next action

Combine potatoes, 2 cups milk and 1 tsp salt in a covered saucepan and bring to a boil. Add cauliflower and scallion whites; simmer covered 10–15 minutes until tender. Add remaining milk and puree. Season to taste. Crisp and crumble bacon; top each bowl with cheese, sour cream, bacon and scallion greens.
