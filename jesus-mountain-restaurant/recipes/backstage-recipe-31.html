Ephramic Lentil-Quinoa Soup

Provisional kitchen prototype. Ephramic title in Drive; complete family formula not confirmed. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

1 cup dry lentils; ½ cup dry quinoa; 1 onion; 2 carrots; 2 celery stalks; 1 can diced tomatoes; 6 cups broth; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tbsp oil.

Method

Sauté diced vegetables in oil. Add rinsed lentils, quinoa, tomatoes, broth and cumin; simmer 30–35 minutes until tender. Season.
