Muller Hot-Sour Soup

Soups · Recovered family source — working draft

Family source includes smaller bracketed quantities and a freezer prep system for bamboo shoots and tofu. Chicken must be thoroughly cooked.

Ingredients

12 cups water; 2 tbsp Better Than Bouillon; ½ cup dried wood ear strips; 6 dried shiitake mushrooms; 1 square inch ginger; ⅓ cup red wine vinegar; ⅔ cup soy sauce; 1 tbsp fish sauce; 1 tsp sesame oil; ½ tbsp chili paste; optional 2 tbsp butter; 1 egg; 4 oz firm tofu; ⅓ cup bamboo shoots; 3 baby carrots; ⅛ lb chicken; pinch sugar; ⅓ cup cornstarch mixed with equal water.

How to prepare

Bring water and seasonings to a boil, simmer mushrooms and wood ears until tender. Drizzle egg into simmering broth without stirring immediately. Add tofu, bamboo, carrots and cooked chicken; slice softened ingredients thinly as needed. Stir in cornstarch slurry to thicken.

Kitchen planning

Use this recipe to plan pantry, freezer and fresh purchases. Check what is already in stock before buying. Record family-tested yield, price, preferred supplier, freezer suitability and variations after cooking.
