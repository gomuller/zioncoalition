Raspberry Vinaigrette

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

1 cup raspberries; 1 tbsp sugar; ⅔ cup balsamic vinegar; ¼ cup olive oil; 1 tbsp honey; ½ tsp salt.

Method

Macerate raspberries with sugar 10 minutes; mash. Shake with vinegar, oil, honey and salt in a lidded jar. Refrigerate.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
