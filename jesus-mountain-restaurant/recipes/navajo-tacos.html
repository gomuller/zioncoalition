Navajo Tacos — Trial

Saturday · Provisional trial recipe — NOT verified family original

Provisional frybread and topping recipe; original Muller Navajo taco recipe not located.

Ingredients

2 cups flour; 2 tsp baking powder; ½ tsp salt; about ¾ cup warm water; oil for frying; 2 cups cooked beans or chili; lettuce, tomatoes, cheese and salsa.

How to prepare

Mix flour, baking powder, salt and water to a soft dough. Rest 15 minutes; divide and flatten into disks. Fry carefully in hot oil until golden and cooked through. Drain; top with hot beans or chili and fresh toppings.
