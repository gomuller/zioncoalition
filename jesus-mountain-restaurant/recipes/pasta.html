Family Pasta — Trial Recipe

Sunday · Provisional trial recipe — NOT verified family original

Provisional draft; not asserted to be the family’s established pasta recipe.

Ingredients

12 oz dry pasta; 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes; 2 cloves garlic; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp dried basil; salt and pepper; optional grated cheese.

How to prepare

Cook pasta in boiling water according to package directions. Warm garlic in oil, add tomatoes and basil and simmer 10–15 minutes. Toss pasta with sauce and top with cheese if desired.

Kitchen planning

Use this recipe to plan pantry, freezer and fresh purchases. Check what is already in stock before buying. Record family-tested yield, price, preferred supplier, freezer suitability and variations after cooking.
