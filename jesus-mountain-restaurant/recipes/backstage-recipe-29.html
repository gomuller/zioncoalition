Rice and Beans

Family archive-derived working draft. family notes identify pairing; quantities provisional. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

2 cups cooked rice; 2 cups cooked black or pinto beans; 1 diced onion; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tbsp oil; ½ cup salsa.

Method

Sauté onion in oil with cumin. Add beans and salsa; simmer 5 minutes. Serve over rice.
