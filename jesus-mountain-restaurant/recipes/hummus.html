Muller Hummus

Pita accompaniments · Recovered family source — working draft

From the pita/hummus section of Greg recipes. Water adjustment is a provisional kitchen technique.

Ingredients

1 can garbanzo beans; 1 tbsp lemon juice; 1 tbsp sesame seeds or a little sesame oil; salt to taste.

How to prepare

Drain chickpeas; blend or mash with lemon juice, sesame and salt, adjusting consistency with a little water as needed.
