Pumpkin / Butternut Squash Soup

TESTABLE PROTOTYPE — not an authenticated Muller family recipe. Source research: original recipe / technique reference. The source recipe was developed for butternut squash. Pumpkin is Greg's approved interchangeable ingredient, but the pumpkin version has not been tested in this kitchen.

Yield and time: 6 servings; about 45 minutes

Ingredients

2 tbsp olive oil; 1 chopped yellow onion; ½ tsp salt; 3 lb peeled and cubed butternut squash OR an equivalent weight of prepared cooking pumpkin; 3 chopped garlic cloves; 1 tbsp chopped sage; ½ tbsp rosemary; 1 tsp grated ginger; 3–4 cups vegetable broth; black pepper. Optional parsley and toasted pumpkin seeds.

Method

Sauté onion in oil with salt until soft, about 5–8 minutes. Add squash or pumpkin and cook another 8–10 minutes. Stir in garlic, herbs and ginger for 1 minute. Add 3 cups broth; simmer covered until tender, 20–30 minutes. Blend until smooth, thinning with remaining broth as needed. Adjust salt and pepper.
