Jenny’s Juju Peach Cobbler

Recovered family archive — working transcription. Not yet retested or approved for the current restaurant menu. Family source preserved below.

Ingredients

2 jars bottled peaches, drained; ¼ cup white sugar plus ¼ cup brown sugar; ¼ tsp cinnamon; ⅛ tsp nutmeg; 1 tsp lemon juice; 2 tsp cornstarch. Topping: 1 cup flour; ¼ cup white sugar; ¼ cup brown sugar; 1 tsp baking powder; ¼ tsp salt; 6 tbsp cold butter; ¼ cup boiling water. Finish: 3 tbsp sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon.

Method

Heat oven to 425°F. Toss peaches with sugars, cinnamon, nutmeg, lemon juice and cornstarch; bake in a 2-quart dish for 10 minutes. Mix topping dry ingredients, cut in butter, stir in hot water. Spoon topping over peaches, sprinkle with cinnamon sugar and bake approximately 20 minutes until golden.
