Egg-Drop Soup

Recovered family archive — working transcription. Not yet retested or approved for the current restaurant menu. Family source preserved below.

Ingredients

4 cups chicken broth (reserve ¾ cup); ⅛ tsp ground ginger; 2 tbsp chopped chives; ¼ tsp salt; 1½ tbsp cornstarch; 2 eggs plus 1 egg yolk.

Method

Bring remaining broth to a boil with ginger, chives and salt. Whisk reserved broth with cornstarch. Beat eggs and yolk; slowly drizzle into simmering broth to form ribbons. Stir in cornstarch slurry gradually to desired thickness.
