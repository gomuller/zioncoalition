Chana Masala

Family archive-derived working draft. family Drive chana-masala and matar-paneer notes, simplified working draft. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

2 cups cooked chickpeas; 1 diced onion; 2 minced garlic cloves; 1 tsp ginger; 1 tsp cumin; ½ tsp coriander; ½ tsp garam masala; ¼ tsp turmeric; 1 can tomatoes; 2 tbsp butter; salt.

Method

Sauté onion in butter; add garlic, ginger and spices for 1 minute. Add tomatoes, simmer 10 minutes. Add chickpeas and simmer 10 minutes more; salt to taste.

Family sources: Greg Recipes, Food Notes, Food Room. For non-family prototypes, an independently tested published source still needs verification; do not treat this as a sourced external recipe.
