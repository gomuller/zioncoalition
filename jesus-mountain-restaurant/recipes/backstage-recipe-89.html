Aunt Sonia’s Oatmeal Cookies

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

1 cup butter; 1 cup white sugar; 1 cup brown sugar; 2 eggs; 1 tsp vanilla; 2 cups flour; 1 tsp baking soda; 1 tsp salt; 2 tsp cinnamon; 3 cups oats; 1 cup raisins or craisins; 1 cup coconut.

Method

Mix butter and sugars, add eggs and vanilla, then dry ingredients, oats, fruit and coconut. Chill 1 hour. Bake at 375°F for about 8 minutes; cool before storing.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
