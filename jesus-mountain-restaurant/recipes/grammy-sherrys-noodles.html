Ingredients

12 oz noodles; 2 tbsp soy sauce; 1 tbsp neutral oil; 1 tsp sesame oil; 2 cloves garlic; 2 cups thinly sliced vegetables; optional chopped scallions.

How to prepare

Cook noodles according to package directions. Sauté garlic and vegetables until tender. Add noodles, soy sauce and sesame oil; toss until hot.
