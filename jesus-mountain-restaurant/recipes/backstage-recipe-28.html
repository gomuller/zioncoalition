Som Tum

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; family som tum unspecified. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

1 green papaya (about 3 cups shredded); 1 carrot; 2 tomatoes; 2 tbsp lime juice; 1 tbsp fish sauce; 1 tbsp palm or brown sugar; 1 minced garlic clove; 1 chile; ¼ cup peanuts.

Method

Pound garlic and chile gently; mix with lime, fish sauce and sugar. Toss with shredded papaya, carrot and tomatoes. Top with peanuts.
