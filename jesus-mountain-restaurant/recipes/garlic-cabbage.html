Garlic Cabbage

Working recipe · approximately four servings · family testing invited

Ingredients

½ medium cabbage; 1 tbsp oil; 3 cloves garlic; 1–2 tsp soy sauce; black pepper

Method

Slice cabbage. Stir-fry in oil 5–7 minutes, adding a splash of water if needed. Add minced garlic for the final minute; season with soy sauce and pepper.

Shopping and storage logic

Cabbage is versatile and keeps well refrigerated.
