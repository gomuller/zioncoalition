Classic Ranch Dressing

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

1 minced garlic clove; 2 tbsp Worcestershire; 2 tbsp white wine vinegar; ⅔ cup mayonnaise; ⅔ cup sour cream; ⅔ cup whole milk; 2 tbsp each parsley, dill and chives.

Method

Whisk everything together; chill before serving.
