Pitas with Black Bean Salad

Saturday · Historical Muller family recipe · Awaiting family verification

The recipe comes from the ‘pita & sop’ section of Big Al’s pizza document. Verify the underlying bread dough recipe.

Ingredients

Pita: ½ batch risen whole-wheat bread dough, cornmeal. Salad: 1 can (14.5 oz) drained black beans; 1 finely chopped onion; 2 Roma tomatoes; ½ green pepper; ½ cucumber; ¼ cup parsley; 1 tbsp fresh mint (or 1 tsp dried); 2 tbsp feta; juice of ½ lemon; salt; 2 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp balsamic vinegar.

Method

Roll pita dough 1/8–1/16 inch thick; dust lightly with cornmeal and bake on a hot pizza stone at 550°F for approximately 3 minutes. Mix all salad ingredients and chill several hours for flavor. Open pitas and fill with salad.
