Pitas with Black Bean Salad

Saturday dinner · Historical Muller family recipe · Working draft

Pita and salad are from the ‘pita & sop’ portion of the Big Al’s pizza document. The original recipe relies on an unstated whole-wheat bread formula; pita count and salad yield were not recorded.

Ingredients

IngredientQuantity in family source
Risen whole-wheat bread dough½ batch; dough formula pending
CornmealFor dusting
Black beans1 can, 14.5 ounces, drained
Onion1, finely chopped
Roma tomatoes2 (or 1 large tomato)
Green pepper½, diced
Cucumber½, diced
Parsley¼ cup, minced
Mint1 tablespoon fresh, or 1 teaspoon dried
Feta cheese2 tablespoons, grated
LemonJuice of ½
SaltTo taste
Olive oil2 tablespoons
Balsamic vinegar1 teaspoon

Preparation

  1. Prepare and rise half a batch of the family whole-wheat bread dough.
  2. Roll pita dough ⅛–1/16 inch thick; lightly coat with cornmeal and place on a generously cornmeal-dusted baking sheet.
  3. Carefully transfer to a hot pizza stone without collapsing the dough; bake at 550°F for approximately 3 minutes. Use care with the very hot oven and stone.
  4. Mix the drained black beans, diced vegetables, herbs, feta, lemon juice, salt, olive oil and balsamic vinegar.
  5. Refrigerate the salad for several hours to develop flavor. Cut open pitas and fill just before serving.

Preparation, storage and kits

Dry pantry kit: beans, cornmeal, olive oil and vinegar. Fresh kit: tomatoes, cucumber, herbs, lemon and feta. Keep salad chilled; fill pitas only when ready to eat to prevent sogginess.

Why we buy and prepare it this way

This recipe reuses the same mother dough as Friday pizza, a useful example of shared shopping and preparation across the weekly menu.

Store perishable ingredients promptly at 40°F or below and frozen items at 0°F or below. Refrigerate prepared foods within two hours. These are general food-safety guidelines, not claims from the historical recipe.
