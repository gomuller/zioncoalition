Cornbread

External-source-inspired testable prototype — not the historical family recipe. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

1 cup cornmeal; 1 cup flour; 1 tbsp baking powder; ½ tsp salt; ¼ cup sugar; 1 cup milk; 1 egg; ¼ cup melted butter.

Method

Heat oven to 400°F. Whisk dry ingredients, then wet ingredients separately. Combine without overmixing. Pour into greased 8-inch square pan and bake 20–25 minutes until golden and center tests clean.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
