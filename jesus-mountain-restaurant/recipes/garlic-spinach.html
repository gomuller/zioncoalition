Garlic Spinach

Working recipe · approximately four servings · family testing invited

Ingredients

1 lb frozen spinach; 1 tbsp oil; 2 garlic cloves; soy sauce or salt to taste; optional lemon juice

Method

Thaw spinach enough to separate and drain excess liquid. Heat oil, briefly cook garlic, then add spinach and sauté 4–6 minutes until hot. Season to taste.

Shopping and storage logic

Frozen spinach stores well and reduces waste.
