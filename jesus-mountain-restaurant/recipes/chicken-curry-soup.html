Chicken Curry

TESTABLE PROTOTYPE — not an authenticated Muller family recipe. Source research: original recipe / technique reference. Family prototype adapted from RecipeTin Eats' approachable mild curry concept, not its exact published formula. The yellow list says 'curry–chicken'; whether it is meant as soup or entrée remains open.

Yield and time: 4 servings; about 30 minutes

Ingredients

1½ tbsp neutral oil; 2 minced garlic cloves; 2 tsp grated ginger; ½ chopped onion; 1 lb sliced boneless chicken thighs; 2½ tbsp mild curry powder; 1 cup chicken broth; 1¼ cups canned crushed tomatoes; ½ cup coconut milk or cream; salt to taste; chopped cilantro optional.

Method

Sauté onion in oil until soft; add garlic and ginger for 1 minute. Add chicken and curry powder and stir until coated and lightly colored. Add broth and tomatoes; simmer 12–15 minutes until chicken is cooked through to 165°F. Stir in coconut milk and simmer 2–3 minutes more; season and serve with rice or naan.
