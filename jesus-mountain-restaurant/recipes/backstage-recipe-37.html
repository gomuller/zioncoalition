Tom Yum Soup

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; family Tom Yum unspecified. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

4 cups chicken broth; 2 cups water; 2 stalks lemongrass; 4 slices galangal or ginger; 4 lime leaves (optional); 8 oz mushrooms; 8 oz shrimp or tofu; 2 tbsp fish sauce; 2 tbsp lime juice; 1 tsp chili paste.

Method

Simmer aromatics in broth and water 10 minutes. Add mushrooms and protein; cook until done (shrimp opaque). Turn off heat, add fish sauce, lime and chili paste; strain woody aromatics.
