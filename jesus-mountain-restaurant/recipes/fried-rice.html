Family Fried Rice — Trial Recipe

Sunday · Provisional trial recipe — NOT verified family original

Provisional draft: no exact Muller family fried-rice recipe was identified in the reviewed source documents. Cool and refrigerate cooked rice promptly, use within 3–4 days, and reheat thoroughly.

Ingredients

3 cups chilled cooked rice; 2 eggs; 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables; 2 tbsp soy sauce; 1 tbsp neutral oil; 2 sliced scallions (optional); 1 tsp sesame oil (optional).

How to prepare

Heat oil in a large skillet, scramble eggs until fully set, and set aside. Cook vegetables until hot. Add chilled rice and stir-fry until steaming throughout. Add soy sauce, return eggs, and finish with optional scallions and sesame oil.
