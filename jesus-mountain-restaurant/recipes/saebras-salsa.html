Saebra’s Salsa

Sides / Tacos · Recovered family source — working draft

Original lists optional lime, tomatillos, frozen cilantro and peppers, or bottled lemon juice.

Ingredients

4 cans (14.5 oz each) stewed tomatoes; 1 large onion; 1 green pepper; 1 small Anaheim pepper; 1 jalapeño; 1 tbsp cilantro; juice of 1 lemon; ½ tsp salt; ½ tsp minced garlic; ⅛ tsp black pepper.

How to prepare

Chop peppers and onion. Whisk stewed tomatoes repeatedly to desired texture (the family note says about 500 whisks), then mix everything.

Kitchen planning

Use this recipe to plan pantry, freezer and fresh purchases. Check what is already in stock before buying. Record family-tested yield, price, preferred supplier, freezer suitability and variations after cooking.
