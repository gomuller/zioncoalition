Mint Chutney

Recovered family notes — working transcription. Family tasting, yield and any adjustments are pending. View source.

Ingredients

1 bunch cilantro; 1½ cups mint leaves; 1 green chile; ½ tsp salt; 1 medium onion; 1 tbsp lemon or tamarind juice; up to ¼ cup water.

Method

Process herbs, chile, onion, salt and juice into a paste, adding water gradually until thick and spoonable.

Development notes

Record yield, taste adjustments, preparation time and freezer suitability after testing.
