Gyros

Provisional kitchen prototype. trial interpretation; not traditional spit-roasted gyro. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

1½ lb ground lamb or beef; 1 tsp oregano; 1 tsp cumin; 2 minced garlic cloves; ½ tsp salt; 6 pitas; cucumber; tomato; yogurt sauce.

Method

Mix meat with seasonings; form thin patties. Cook to safe temperature (160°F for ground beef/lamb). Slice, stuff into warm pitas with vegetables and yogurt sauce.

Archive and testing

Family sources: Greg Recipes, Food Notes, Food Room. For non-family prototypes, an independently tested published source still needs verification; do not treat this as a sourced external recipe.
