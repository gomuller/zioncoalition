BLT Sandwiches

Family archive-derived working draft. family archive mentions BLT sandwich, exact quantities provisional. This page is not yet family-tested or approved for front-stage service. Where the archive names a dish without a complete formula, quantities are a test formulation, not a claimed original.

Ingredients

8 slices bread; 8 slices cooked bacon; 1 tomato; 4 lettuce leaves; 4 tbsp mayonnaise.

Method

Toast bread, spread with mayonnaise, layer bacon, tomato and lettuce; close and cut.
