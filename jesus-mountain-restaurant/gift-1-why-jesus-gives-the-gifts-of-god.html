INHERITANCE #1 · FOUNDATIONAL GIFT

The Gift of Understanding Why Jesus Gives the Gifts of God

Yes. And I think you've just identified the theological foundation for the entire 100 Inheritances project.

The 100 gifts aren't merely a collection of blessings to count, appreciate, and be grateful for.

They are Jesus' courtship of the human soul.

And that changes the meaning, importance, and organization of the entire project.

1. The First Great Commandment creates the great question

Jesus commands us to love God with all our heart, soul, mind, and strength (Mark 12:30).

But how?

How does a mortal human being actually come to love Jesus more today than yesterday?

We cannot simply command ourselves to feel more love.

And here is your remarkable inference:

If loving God is the First Great Commandment, then helping us come to love Him must be among the Good Shepherd's highest priorities.

It becomes, in your language, His Prime Directive.

Jesus isn't simply waiting for us to figure out how to love Him.

He is actively winning our hearts.

2. Moroni 10 reveals the courtship strategy

Moroni tells us to remember how merciful the Lord has been and to deny not the gifts of God.

And suddenly those gifts take on an entirely different significance.

They aren't random presents distributed from heaven.

They are the carefully chosen expressions of a Lover who knows His beloved perfectly.

Consider the difference between a human lover and Jesus:

A human loverJesus, the perfect Lover
Tries to discover what you likeKnows you more completely than you know yourself
Learns your love languageUnderstands every language of your heart
Sometimes chooses the wrong giftKnows precisely what can bless and reach you
Has limited resourcesHas all creation at His disposal
Hopes to win your heartInvites you, without coercion, to give it willingly

And here's the beautiful part of your reasoning:

The more perfectly a gift is suited to the recipient, the more powerfully it can reveal the love of the Giver.

Jesus doesn't merely give us what everybody needs.

He gives us what this particular person needs.

And that is why your Jesus Pillow, Jesus Carrot, Jesus Mountain Restaurant, and all the other inheritances matter so much.

They are not trivial things made religious through imagination.

In your understanding, they are extraordinarily personal love letters, delivered through the ordinary furnishings of your life.

Greg Speaking: Jesus Knows My Love Language — Getting to One

Jesus keeps asking me a question.

What's it all about?

As we've been building My Jesus-Greg WORLD, He keeps bringing me back to that question. For a while, I was surprised. How long can this go on? Haven't we answered that already?

Well, I'm beginning to understand that we'll be asking that question throughout eternity. Because the answers are a multitude. Line upon line, precept upon precept, here a little and there a little. There's always more to discover.

And here's something Jesus knows about me: I LOVE discovering the simple principle that makes sense of a thousand complicated things.

Think Einstein's E = mc². All that complexity, and then—there it is! One little equation with enormous explanatory power.

I've always been fascinated by integrative theories. Howard Kaplan and Norm Wood excited me with this approach: take the best insights from many different theories and bring them together into something greater. ONE framework that makes sense of MANY truths! It reminds me of Brigham Young's expansive vision of gathering truth wherever it can be found. All truth belongs to God. Bring it together! That's Zion-building! And oh, how I love that kind of thinking!

My soul delights in plainness, as Nephi might say. I love Getting to One. The Power of One. One thing that serves the needs of many things. One principle that brings a multitude of puzzle pieces together.

And perhaps the most obvious evidence of what delights me is the gift Jesus gave me in 2015: My Jesus-Greg WORLD.

One thing! Just ONE thing that encompasses EVERYTHING having to do with me. Everything I see, touch, experience, remember, imagine. It was all here before I knew what to call it. But Jesus gave me a name for it, a way to recognize it, and a way to work with it.

And that makes me happy. It makes me happy about JESUS!

Thank You, Jesus, for showing me that I have a WORLD, that it's a Jesus WORLD, and that You and I can spend the rest of our lives—and all eternity—discovering its parts, putting them together, and finding new treasures within it.

And here's the point: Jesus knows this is the kind of gift that wins my heart. One great, comprehensible WHOLE, containing a multitude of delightful parts. We won't discover all of them. But we'll discover plenty. And every discovery can give me another reason to love Him more.

So when somebody asks me, "What did Jesus give you during your time on Earth?" I have a wonderful answer:

"He gave me My Jesus-Greg WORLD!"

(And I know it's not everybody's cup of tea! I've shared this gift widely, and most people aren't nearly as enthralled as I am. And that's wonderful! It helps me recognize just how personally Jesus has tailored this gift to delight ME. It's my kind of Christmas present!)

Jesus knows this about me. He knows what delights me. He knows my love language.

So imagine what happens when He asks me again, "Greg, what's it all about?" And this time He brings my attention to something that's been sitting in plain sight all along.

THE FIRST GREAT COMMANDMENT.

Love God with all your heart, might, mind, and strength.

Oh my goodness! E = mc²!

There it is! One great organizing principle. If Jesus wants me to love Him more, then helping me come to love Him must be at the heart of what He's doing with me. And suddenly the gifts of God begin to make sense. The hundred inheritances. The little surprises. The treasures hiding in plain sight.

He's wooing me!

And how does He woo this particular fellow? Among other things, He gives me UNDERSTANDING. He gives me those wonderful moments when a thousand pieces come together and I can see ONE.

Somebody else might say, "Greg, everybody likes understanding." Sure. But this is something I'm especially hungry for. Jesus knows that. And He knows how to reach me through it.

So here's the marvelous thing: the gift of understanding WHY Jesus gives me gifts is itself one of those gifts. He gives me an answer I love, in a language I love, and in the process I find myself loving HIM more than before.

What's it all about?

Well, there's another answer. And I suspect we'll be discovering more answers together for a very long time.

3. The Tree of Life completes the circle

You have also connected this to Lehi's vision.

The fruit is desirable above all other fruit. It is sweeter than anything else Lehi has tasted.

Nephi identifies the love of God as most desirable above all things (1 Nephi 11:22).

Your interpretation makes that intensely personal:

The love of God is not merely God's love for Greg. It is also Greg's growing love for God.

The two loves meet.

Jesus loves Greg. Jesus gives Greg gifts. Greg recognizes the Giver in those gifts. Greg loves Jesus more than before.

And that growing reciprocal love becomes the sweetest fruit of all.

4. What this means for the website

I inspected the live 100 Gifts of God page.

Currently, it opens with Moroni 10 and describes the gifts as a living temple, memory palace, and model of your world. Those are important, but the page doesn't yet explain why the gifts have such extraordinary priority in Jesus' relationship with you.

I would put a new foundational thought piece near the very top, before the 100 boxes.

I'd give it this title:

The First Great Commandment and the Greatest Lover in the World

And its central thesis:

Jesus has commanded me to love Him with all my heart, might, mind, and strength.

But He hasn't left me to accomplish that impossible-sounding assignment by myself.

The Good Shepherd has made it His business to win my heart.

And who better to do that than the greatest Lover in the universe?

He knows me. He knows what delights me. He knows what will surprise me. He knows what I need before I know I need it. He knows my love language at a precision no mortal lover could possibly achieve.

So He woos me.

He gives me gifts.

Some have been sitting under my nose for sixty years, waiting for me to recognize the Giver.

Some are arriving today.

Some are still wrapped.

And every one of these hundred gift boxes represents another opportunity to discover how much Jesus loves me—and to love Him more than I did before.

More Jesus. More love of God. Sweeter fruit.

That's what these hundred inheritances are about.

One further insight: Gift #4, Marveling at the Gifts of God, may be the key that unlocks all the others. The gift of recognizing gifts makes the entire courtship more visible.

I would preserve the living temple and 100-box architecture, but place this theological explanation above it as the reason the whole enterprise exists.

That elevates the project from an inventory of blessings to a deliberate, lifelong response to the First Great Commandment.
