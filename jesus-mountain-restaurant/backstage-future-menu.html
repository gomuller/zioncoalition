Backstage · Future Menu Development

The family food-history mop-up: all 98 items from our numbered Drive inventory, preserved in their original numbering. This is a working development menu, not public restaurant offerings. We favor original Muller recipes; where missing, external recipes are clearly marked as test candidates. A link to a family document does not mean its recipe has been fully transcribed here. The catalog is a starting point for future family tasting, editing, and approval.

Recipe-page progress: 29 dishes already linked to restaurant recipes; 25 additional backstage recipes now have their own cookable pages (21 recovered from family sources and 4 externally researched prototypes, including prior work). 44 remaining entries still need complete recipe-page development or family clarification. Every item remains visible below; this page does not mislabel incomplete research as a finished recipe.
